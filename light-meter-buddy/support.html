Light Meter Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. It says "Very dim" but I can see fine.

Our eyes are very good in dim light: a room at dusk can be under 5 lux and still look fine. Phone light sensors count in whole lux and round dim light down, so under 5 lux the app shows "under 5 lux" and 🌑 Very dim instead of a number it can't trust.

2. My other phone shows a different number.

Phone light sensors are made for adjusting the screen, so they are approximate: in our test, two phones under the same lamp differed by 15-30%. The plain-words answers use wide bands, so this rarely changes the answer. To compare spots, measure them all with the same phone.

3. Where is the light sensor?

At the top of the screen, next to the front camera. Hold the phone screen up, or facing the window or lamp, and keep your hand and shadow off it.

4. The number keeps changing.

The light is changing (a flickering lamp, clouds) or the phone is moving. The number is the middle of the last 2 seconds; wait for 🟢 Steady, or use ⏸ Hold to freeze a reading.

5. How do I check a spot for a plant?

Open 🪴 Plants, pick how much light your plant needs, and hold the phone at leaf height facing the window, during the day. With Pro, a 📈 Light log records the spot all day and tells you how many hours were bright enough.

6. The light log stopped.

The log only records while the app is open on the screen. Leave the phone at the spot with the app open (plug it in for a long log); the screen goes dark by itself to save the battery. If you leave the app, the log pauses, and that time isn't counted.

7. Can I see foot-candles instead of lux?

Yes: ⚙️ Settings › Units.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

9. Does Light Meter Buddy need any permissions?

It never asks you for any. It reads the light sensor, which needs no permission, and keeps everything on your phone. (Google Play's billing, used for the Pro purchase, adds its own internet permission.) See the Privacy Policy.
