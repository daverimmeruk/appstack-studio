Appliance Cost Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Where do I find my price per kWh?

On your electricity bill or in your supplier's app: the price for one kWh, often called the unit rate or energy charge. Type it with tax, as it is on the bill, on the 🧾 Tariff tab, in the main unit (e.g. £0.245, €0.30) or in pence or cents (e.g. 24.5p, 14¢). The app uses your phone's currency; you can pick another. It never guesses or converts a price: until you type it, you see energy (kWh) only.

2. Where do I find an appliance's watts?

On the rating label or plate (often on the back or underneath), on the plug or charger, or in the manual. 1 kW is 1,000 W. For a washing machine, dishwasher or tumble dryer, the energy label gives kWh per cycle (or per 100 cycles: divide by 100); for a fridge or freezer it gives kWh a year. A plug-in energy meter shows the real use of anything that plugs in.

3. Why is my oven or heater cheaper than the app says?

Appliances with a thermostat (oven, heater, iron, fridge) switch on and off to hold the temperature, so they don't draw full power all the time. Watts and time give the most it can cost. A plug-in energy meter, or the kWh figure on the label, gives a closer figure.

4. Why isn't the fixed charge on my bill included?

A fixed daily or monthly charge (a standing charge) is paid whatever you use, so it isn't part of any one appliance's cost. It's still on your bill.

5. What are the typical figures?

📋 Start from a typical appliance fills in common figures (for example a 3,000 W kettle for 10 minutes a day) as a starting point. They're marked as typical until you change them: the label on your own appliance gives the real figures.

6. Where are my figures kept?

Only in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not in Android's backups. Free keeps up to 5 appliances; Pro keeps as many as you like.

7. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
