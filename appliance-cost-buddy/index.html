📖 Appliance Cost Buddy Manual

Everything below is the same text as the Manual in the app (📖 Manual tab).

👋 What Appliance Cost Buddy does

Appliance Cost Buddy works out what each appliance in your home costs to run: a use, a day, a month and a year, plus the cost of leaving it on standby. The list puts the dearest first and shows each one's share of the total, so you can see where the money goes.

Everything is worked out from the figures you type: the power or energy from the appliance's label, how long you use it, and the price on your bill. The app never guesses your price. Everything stays on your phone: no account and no ads.

🧾 Your price

On 🧾 Tariff, type the price for one kWh (kilowatt hour) from your electricity bill or your supplier's app, with tax, as it is on the bill. Type it as the bill gives it: in pounds, euros, dollars and so on, or in pence or cents (e.g. 24.5p).

The app uses your phone's currency and writes numbers the way your phone does. Change the currency on 🧾 Tariff if you need to; the app never converts prices, so type the price again in the new currency.

Until a price is typed, the app shows energy only (kWh), not costs.

A fixed charge on your bill (a daily or monthly standing charge) isn't part of any appliance's cost: you pay it whatever you use, so the app leaves it out. When your price changes, type the new one and every cost is worked out again.

➕ Adding an appliance

On ⚡ Appliances, tap ➕ Add an appliance and type its name. Then choose how to measure it:

  • Watts and time: the power in watts (W) from the label or the plug, how long it's on each day, and how many days a week. Type 24 hours for something that's always on. 1 kW is 1,000 W.
  • kWh per use: for a washing machine, dishwasher or tumble dryer, the energy for one use (on the label, or from a plug-in meter) and how many times a week.
  • kWh a year: for a fridge or freezer, the figure on its energy label.

📋 Start from a typical appliance fills in typical figures for a kettle, TV, oven and more. They're only a starting point and are marked as typical until you change them: the label on your appliance gives the real figures.

🔌 Standby

Many appliances use a little power when they're off but still plugged in: a TV on standby, a microwave's clock. For watts and time, type the standby watts (from the manual, or a plug-in meter). The app counts it for the rest of the week, when the appliance isn't on.

The card shows the standby cost a year: that's what switching it off at the wall saves.

📋 The list and the totals

At the top of ⚡ Appliances, the total for all your appliances: a year, a month, and the energy in kWh. Each card shows how you use it, the cost a year and a month, its share of the total, and the cost of each hour of use or each use.

Tap a card to ✏️ change it, see 🔮 What if (Pro), or ❌ delete it.

Appliances with a thermostat (oven, heater, iron, fridge) don't draw full power all the time. For them, watts and time give the most it can cost; a plug-in energy meter shows the real use.

🌙 Day and night rates

Some tariffs have a cheaper rate at night, e.g. Economy 7 in the UK. With ⭐ Pro, switch on day and night rates on 🧾 Tariff and type both rates from your bill, and how many hours a day the night rate lasts (Economy 7: 7). Then each appliance has a box for how much of its use is at night (e.g. 100% for a washing machine on a timer at night). Standby runs day and night, so it's counted at the night rate for those hours and at the day rate for the rest.

Your night hours are on your bill or your supplier's website.

🔮 What if

With ⭐ Pro, tap an appliance and choose 🔮 What if. Change the time, the days, the watts (e.g. a newer model) or switch off standby, and see the new cost a year and the saving straight away. Nothing is changed until you tap Keep the new figures.

⭐ Free and Pro

Free: up to 5 appliances, your price and currency, costs a use, a day, a month and a year, standby costs, typical figures and the totals. No ads.

Pro (one payment, no subscription): as many appliances as you like, 🌙 day and night rates (e.g. Economy 7), 🔮 What if, 📄 a running-cost report (PDF) and 📊 a spreadsheet (CSV) for Excel or Google Sheets.

Tap GO PRO at the top. Restore purchase gets Pro back on a new phone with the same Google account.

🔒 Privacy

No account, no sign-in, and no ads. Your appliances and your tariff are kept only in the app's own storage on this phone. Appliance Cost Buddy has no servers and needs no permissions apart from Google Play's for the Pro purchase. Something only leaves the phone when you share it.

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored. Google Play handles the Pro purchase.
