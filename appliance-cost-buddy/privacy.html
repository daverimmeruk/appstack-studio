Appliance Cost Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.appliancecostbuddy | Effective Date: 27 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Appliance Cost Buddy works out what your appliances cost to run from the figures you type. Your figures stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic information, see section 4.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Appliances: the name you type and its figures: watts, how long it's on, days a week and standby watts, or the energy per use and uses a week, or the energy a year from its label, and (with Economy 7) how much of its use is at night.
  • Tariff: the price you type from your bill, the currency you chose (if not your phone's), and with Pro the day and night rates and night hours.
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many useful results you have had (used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating).

3. Permissions Used

  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No other permission: no camera, storage, contacts, location, phone, SMS or notifications.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example an appliance added or a report made), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

5. Sharing

Something only leaves your phone when you choose to share it: the running-cost report or the spreadsheet (Pro), or the manual.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete an appliance from its pop-up.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Appliance Cost Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

7. Children

Appliance Cost Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
