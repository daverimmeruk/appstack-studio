Pantry Expiry Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. What's the difference between use by and best before?

"Use by" is about safety: don't eat food after its use-by date, even if it looks and smells fine. "Best before" is about quality: food is often fine after it, so check how it looks and smells first. Always follow the storage instructions on the pack.

2. Why doesn't the app ask for the camera?

The camera screen is Google's code scanner, part of Google Play services. It reads the barcode and hands the app only the number, so the app itself doesn't need camera permission. The first time, Android may need a moment and an internet connection to get the scanner ready.

3. Why does it ask me for the name after scanning?

Barcodes are never looked up online, so the app doesn't know a product the first time. Type the name once: it's remembered on your phone, and the next scan of the same barcode fills in the name and place.

4. I didn't get the morning reminder.

The note only comes when food is due today or tomorrow, or is past its date. Check that the reminder is on at the bottom of the Pantry tab, and that Android allows the app's notifications (Settings › Apps › Pantry Expiry Buddy › Notifications). The time is approximate: Android groups reminders to save battery.

5. Where is my list kept?

Only in the app's own storage on your phone. It is not in Android's backups. Free keeps up to 30 foods at a time; Pro keeps as many as you like.

6. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
