Pantry Expiry Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.pantryexpirybuddy | Effective Date: 27 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Pantry Expiry Buddy keeps a list of the food in your kitchen with the date on each pack. Your list stays on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play services and Google Play's billing library send Google their own diagnostic information, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Foods: the name, amount, place (fridge, freezer or cupboard), the date and whether it's use by or best before, and the barcode if you scanned one.
  • Remembered barcodes: the name and place you typed for a barcode, so the next scan fills them in.
  • Tally: what you marked as used or thrown away, with the date, for the monthly count and the Pro report.
  • Shopping list: the items you add.
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, the reminder switch, the place filter, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many useful results you have had (used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating).

3. Barcode Scanning

The camera screen is Google's code scanner (Google ML Kit), part of Google Play services on your phone. It runs on the phone: the app only receives the number in the barcode, never the camera picture, and has no camera permission. Barcodes are never looked up online. The first time, Google Play services may download the scanner to your phone.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the morning "use soon" reminder. Android asks you first, and you can switch it off in the app.
  • Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): puts the morning reminder back after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No camera, storage, contacts, location, phone, SMS or exact-alarm permission.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google ML Kit (in Google Play services): the code scanner. Google states that ML Kit may send Google information about how well these features work (for example which device and how long a task took), but not the pictures or what the codes say. See Google's ML Kit terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a food added or used), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. Sharing

Something only leaves your phone when you choose to share it: the shopping list as text, the printable list, the used and wasted report or the spreadsheet (Pro), or the manual.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a food from its pop-up, and remove shopping items by pressing and holding them.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Pantry Expiry Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Pantry Expiry Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
