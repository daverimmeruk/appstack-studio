Pill Reminder Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. The alarm didn't ring.

  • Open the app: if it shows a red or yellow box (notifications off, or Alarms & reminders off), tap its button and allow it.
  • Check the alarm's switch on the main screen is on.
  • Samsung phones: Settings › Battery › Background usage limits: make sure Pill Reminder Buddy isn't in Sleeping apps or Deep sleeping apps.
  • Then tap 🔔 Test alarm and lock the phone: it should ring a minute later.

2. The alarm is too quiet.

The alarm plays at the phone's alarm volume. Turn the alarm volume up in the phone's sound settings, or switch on ⚙️ Settings › Extra loud: each ring then turns the alarm volume to full and puts it back afterwards.

3. How do I change the alarm tone?

⚙️ Settings › Change the alarm tone. Tap a tone to hear it: the one you tap becomes your alarm tone.

4. How do I make it say the medicine's name?

Type the name when you set the alarm (or tap the alarm to change it), then choose ⚙️ Settings › How reminders sound › Voice or Tune and voice. It then says, for example, "Time to take your Beta Blocker pills".

5. How do I stop the alarm?

Tap the alarm to open the big screen, then tap the big red STOP ALARM button. The alarm's own STOP ALARM button does the same (on a locked phone, unlock it first). If nobody stops it, it stops by itself about 2 minutes after the first ring.

6. I take a medicine twice a day.

When you set the alarm, tap ＋ Another time the same day and pick the second time. Or set a second alarm with ＋ Set an alarm.

7. Can I turn an alarm off for a while?

Yes: use the switch next to it on the main screen. Turn it on again when you need it.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

9. I'm getting a new phone.

With Pro, use ⚙️ Settings › Save a backup file and keep the file somewhere you can reach from the new phone (for example Google Drive). On the new phone, install Pill Reminder Buddy and use Restore from a backup file.

10. Does Pill Reminder Buddy send my medicines anywhere?

No. Everything stays on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.
