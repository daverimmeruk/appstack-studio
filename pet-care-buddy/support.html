Pet Care Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. How often should my pet have flea, worming or vaccination treatments?

It depends on the product, your pet and where you live, so ask your vet. The app offers usual starting points (for a dog or cat: flea treatment every month, worming every 3 months, a yearly booster and check-up) that you can change to what your vet advises.

2. The treatment was done last week. How do I tell the app?

Tap the reminder (on 🐾 Today, or the pet › 📅 Care reminders) › 📅 Done on another day, and pick the date. The next date counts on from then. When adding a reminder you can also set Last done to a date.

3. I didn't get the morning note.

The note comes at about 09:00 on days something is due, once a day. Check 📄 Reports › Reminders: if it says notifications are blocked, tap it to allow them. Battery savers can delay it; it arrives when the phone wakes.

4. Can I use pounds and another currency?

Yes. 📄 Reports › Units and currency: switch weights between kg and lb (saved weights are converted for you), and pick a currency (amounts keep the numbers you typed: they're never converted).

5. Where are my pets' details kept?

Only in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not in Android's backups, so share the sitter sheet or a pet record (Pro) now and then if you'd like a copy. Free keeps 2 pets; Pro keeps as many as you like.

6. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
