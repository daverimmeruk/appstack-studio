Pet Care Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.petcarebuddy | Effective Date: 28 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Pet Care Buddy keeps your pets' care reminders, weights, costs and photos. Everything stays on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic information, see section 4.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Pets: what you type: name, kind, breed, year of birth, microchip number, vet, food and notes.
  • Care reminders: each reminder's name, how often, when it was done and the dates it was done before.
  • Weights and costs: the weights, amounts, what they were for and their dates.
  • Photos: the photos you take or choose, stored as smaller copies inside the app.
  • Settings: the line for the sitter sheet, kg or lb, the currency you chose (if not your phone's), reminders on or off, whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many useful results you have had (used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating).

3. Permissions Used

  • Notifications: for the one morning note on days care is due. You can switch it off in the app or in Android's settings.
  • Run at start-up: to set the morning reminder again after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No camera, storage, contacts, location, phone or SMS permission. Photos come from your own camera app or Android's photo picker, which hand the app only the photo you choose.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a pet added, care done or a sheet made), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

5. Sharing

Something only leaves your phone when you choose to share it: the sitter sheet, a pet record or the spreadsheet (Pro), or the manual.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a pet, a reminder, a weight, a cost or a photo from its pop-up.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Pet Care Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

7. Children

Pet Care Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
