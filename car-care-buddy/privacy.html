Car Care Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.carcarebuddy | Effective Date: 19 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Car Care Buddy keeps your car's due dates and a log of its costs. Your data and photos stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, doesn't look up your registration, and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic logs, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Cars: the name, the registration if you type it, and miles or km.
  • Due dates: what is due (MOT, road tax, insurance renewal, service, breakdown cover, warranty or other), the date and a note.
  • Log: the date, kind, cost, mileage, litres (fuel), a note, and the receipt photo you take or choose (stored as a smaller copy, at most 1600 pixels across).
  • Settings: whether reminders are on, which reminders were already shown, the car shown, whether the welcome guide was shown and whether Pro is unlocked.

3. Photos

Photos come from your phone's own camera app or from Android's photo picker, which only gives the app the picture you choose. The app has no camera or storage permission and never reads your other photos.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the due-date reminders. Android asks you first.
  • Start after restart (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED): only to set the daily reminder check again after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No location, contacts, microphone, SMS or call log, no exact-alarm permission, no overlay, no accessibility service and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example an entry logged or a PDF made), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. PDFs, Spreadsheet and Manual

Your data only leaves your phone when you share a PDF, the spreadsheet (Pro) or the manual. It is then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a due date by tapping it, or a log entry by tapping it. Delete a car with all its dates, log and photos in ⚙️ Car.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Car Care Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Car Care Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
