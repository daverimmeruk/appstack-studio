Car Care Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Where do I find my inspection date?

It's on your last inspection certificate or report (in the UK, your last MOT certificate: a car's first MOT is due 3 years after it was first registered). Your insurance renewal date is on your policy.

2. When will I be reminded?

At about 09:00, 30 days, 7 days and 1 day before each due date, and on the day. Check that reminders are on in 📄 Reports. If Android blocks the app's notifications, 📄 Reports shows 🔕 Notifications are blocked: tap it to allow them.

3. Why is there no fuel economy yet?

It needs two full tanks with the mileage typed in. Part fills in between are added to the next full tank.

4. My fuel economy looks too good.

A fill-up was probably not logged. Keep logging every fill-up and the average evens out. A fill-up logged without the mileage starts the count again from the next full tank with a mileage.

5. Can I use km and litres per 100 km?

Yes. In ⚙️ Car, tick "Mileage in km": distances show in km and economy in l/100 km.

6. Does it check my inspection or tax online?

No. The app doesn't look anything up and doesn't send your registration anywhere. The dates are the ones you type in.

7. It shows another country's names (MOT, NCT …) or the wrong currency.

The country comes from your SIM card and the currency from your phone's settings. Tap Change the country or Change the currency in 📄 Reports. The app never converts amounts.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
