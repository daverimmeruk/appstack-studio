Phone Check Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.phonecheckbuddy | Effective Date: 15 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Phone Check Buddy reads a phone's spec, tests its parts and produces a report signed by the phone's own security chip, so anyone checking a used phone can trust it. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics. A report only leaves the phone when you make or answer a check code, and it is locked with that code before it is sent, so we cannot read it.

2. What the test reads

  • The phone's hardware and settings: make and model, Android version, build and security update, processor, memory (RAM), storage size and free space, screen, cameras, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth features, NFC, SIM slots, battery status and charge, whether developer options are on, whether fingerprint or face unlock is set up, and signs of rooting.
  • Test measurements: how loud a test tone is at each microphone, how much the phone shakes when it vibrates, the brightness and detail of each camera's picture, sensor readings, the Wi-Fi signal strength, whether the SIM works and the mobile network's name, GPS accuracy and the number of satellites used, and how many Bluetooth devices were heard and the strongest signal.
  • Sound and pictures are never saved or sent. The microphone only listens during the tone test and each camera only opens during its test; only the result (for example "Tone 90 dB above room noise" or "brightness 117/255") is kept.
  • Location is never saved or sent. The GPS test only checks that the phone can find its position; only the accuracy and the number of satellites are kept, not where the phone is.
  • The app never reads photos, messages, contacts, call logs, accounts, files, the IMEI, Wi-Fi network names, or the names or addresses of Bluetooth devices.

3. Check codes and the check service

  • When you make or answer a check code, the phone sends a report to our check service: the spec sheet and test results described above, plus security certificates from the phone's security chip that prove the report came from that phone and hasn't been changed.
  • Before it leaves the phone, the report is locked (AES-256 encryption) with a key made from the check code. The check service only ever holds the locked report, stored under a fingerprint of the code, so neither we nor our service provider can read it without the code.
  • Only someone with the code can open the report. Pass your code only to the person who should see or answer the check.
  • A report is deleted from the check service as soon as the code is used to open it. A report that is never opened can't be opened after 7 days and is then removed by the app's automatic tidy-up.
  • A code made with 🛒 Check a phone that has no report yet holds no information about anyone: only that the code exists and when it expires.
  • The check service is Google Cloud Firestore (part of Google Firebase), stored in London (europe-west2). Like any internet service, Google receives the connection's IP address in order to provide it.

4. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Your last full test: the results of ▶ Start full test, and the original battery capacity if you entered it.
  • Your check codes: the codes you made or answered, and the reports you received with them.
  • 📜 History: a list of credits and Pro bought, the codes made with them, reports sent and received, and codes that expired.
  • Purchases and settings: your available credits, whether Pro is unlocked, and whether the welcome guide was shown.

Phone Check Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone.

5. Permissions and Access Used

  • Internet (INTERNET): for check codes, Google's list of withdrawn security keys, and Google Play Billing.
  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS): to measure the test tone from the loudspeaker and the call earpiece. Asked for when the sound test starts.
  • Camera (CAMERA): to check that each camera produces a picture, and to switch the flashlight on in the full test. Asked for when the camera test starts.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): to check that GPS can find a position. Asked for when the GPS test starts. On older Android versions it is also needed for the Bluetooth test.
  • Nearby devices (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, declared "never for location"): to check that Bluetooth can hear nearby devices. Asked for when the Bluetooth test starts.
  • Wi-Fi and network state (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to read the Wi-Fi features and signal strength.
  • Vibration (VIBRATE): for the vibration test.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): only to see whether a fingerprint or face unlock is set up. The app never asks you to scan your fingerprint or face.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle credits and Pro.

If you refuse a permission, that test is shown as not tested and the rest of the test carries on.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy 1 credit (a single purchase) or Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout.
  • Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore): the check service described in section 3. It only holds locked reports.
  • Google's key attestation service: when a report is checked, the app downloads Google's public list of withdrawn security keys (android.googleapis.com). Nothing about you or the report is sent.

See Google's Privacy Policy.

7. PDF Reports

A PDF report is only made when you tap 📄 PDF, and only shared with the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

8. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Phone Check Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored on the phone, including your codes, received reports and 📜 History. 🗑 in 📜 History deletes that line from History.
  • Reports on the check service are deleted as soon as their code is used, and can't be opened after 7 days.
  • We can't read or identify the locked reports, so we hold nothing about you. If you have a question about your data, email us.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it reads, stores or sends, we will update this page and its effective date.
