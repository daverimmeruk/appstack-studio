App-Cleaner Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.appcleanerbuddy | Effective Date: 13 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

App-Cleaner Buddy shows where your phone's storage goes, which apps you no longer use and which apps keep getting bigger. Everything it reads and saves stays on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics.

2. Usage access

  • Android only tells an app how big other apps are, and when they were last used, if you switch on Usage access for it. App-Cleaner Buddy asks you to switch it on for that reason.
  • It uses Usage access only to read, on your phone: each app's size, data and cache, the storage totals (apps, photos, videos, audio), and the last time each app was used.
  • It does not read the content of other apps, your messages, photos, files or browsing.
  • You can switch Usage access off at any time in Android's settings. App-Cleaner Buddy then only shows total and free space.

3. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Daily storage readings: once a day, the free space and the size of each app (with the app's name and package name), used for the history, growth and forecast. Up to 400 days are kept.
  • Settings: your choices (unused period, sort order, low-storage alert level), whether the welcome guide was shown, whether the demo is on, and whether Pro is unlocked.

4. Permissions and Access Used

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): to read app sizes and last-use times, as described above. Switched on by you in Android's settings.
  • Request uninstall (REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES): to open Android's own "Uninstall this app?" screen when you tap Uninstall. Android asks you to confirm; App-Cleaner Buddy never removes anything by itself.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the Pro low-storage alert, if you turn it on.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, photos or files. It lists the apps in your app drawer without the "all apps" permission.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. CSV and PDF Exports

Your app list and readings only leave your phone when you tap CSV or PDF (Pro, or with the demo's sample data) and share the file. They are then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › App-Cleaner Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored, including the daily readings.
  • App-Cleaner Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone, so it stays on this phone only.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it stores or shares, we will update this page and its effective date.
