❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


Q1: Why does it ask for Usage access?

Android only tells an app how big other apps are, and when they were last used, if you switch on Usage access for it. App-Cleaner Buddy uses it only to read app sizes and last-use times on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere. See our Privacy Policy.

Q2: Why does my 128 GB phone show 137 GB?

Some phones count 1 GB = 1,024 MB. App-Cleaner Buddy uses 1 GB = 1,000 MB, so 128 × 1,024 MB shows as 137 GB. The app says so under the free space when that's the case.

Q3: Can it delete apps, photos or junk for me?

No. Uninstall opens Android's own "Uninstall this app?" screen, so you always confirm. Google Play only lets gallery and file manager apps read all your photos and files, and Android only lets you clear another app's cache yourself in its App info (Storage › Clear cache).

Q4: An app says "No use recorded by Android". What does that mean?

Android keeps usage records for a limited time (often up to about 2 years). If it has no record of an app being used in that time, App-Cleaner Buddy shows the install date instead.

Q5: Why are Growth and the forecast empty?

They are worked out from the daily readings saved on your phone. Growth appears once there are readings from two different days; the forecast needs at least 4 readings over 7 days. Android decides exactly when the daily reading runs, so some days may be missed.

Q6: I didn't get a low-storage alert.

The alert (Pro) is checked once a day, and only if free space is below the level you chose in the Growth tab. On Android 13 and newer, notifications must be allowed for App-Cleaner Buddy (Settings › Apps › App-Cleaner Buddy › Notifications).

Q7: What does Archive do?

On Android 15 and newer, Archive removes the app itself and its cache but keeps its data and logins. The icon stays on your home screen; tap it and Android gets the app back, as it was. Only apps from an app store that supports archiving (such as Google Play) can be archived.

Q8: How do I add the widget?

The widget is part of Pro. Long-press your home screen, tap Widgets, and add App-Cleaner Buddy. It shows free space and the forecast, and updates with the daily reading and whenever you open the app.

Q9: What does Pro include?

Free includes the storage breakdown, unused apps, barely used apps, biggest apps, Archive, the freed total and the demo. Pro adds I need space for…, the guided cache clean, app growth and apps growing in the background, the storage forecast, the low-storage alert, the weekly summary, the home-screen widget, the free-space history graph, CSV export and the PDF report.

Q10: How do I buy or restore Pro?

Pro is one payment for life through Google Play, with no subscription. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. On a new phone with the same Google account, tap GO PRO and then Restore purchase.
