Barometer & Altitude Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Why is my reading lower than the weather report?

Weather reports give the pressure at sea level. Your phone measures the pressure where you are, which is lower the higher up you are (about 1 hPa for every 8 metres). Tap ⛰️ Set your height on 🌡️ Now and the app shows the sea-level pressure to compare.

2. The app says this phone has no pressure sensor.

Not every phone has a barometer. Without one the app can't measure, and nothing is guessed. The 📖 Manual still explains how it works.

3. Why doesn't the graph fill in while the app is closed?

The app measures only while it's open and measuring (▶️ Start to ⏹ Stop); Android doesn't give pressure readings to apps in the background. To follow the pressure for hours or days, leave it measuring on its charger: it saves a reading every 5 minutes and the screen stays on.

4. The height changed although I didn't move.

The weather changes the air pressure slowly, and that looks like a small change in height. Set zero again just before you use it: over a few minutes (stairs, lifts, car parks) it's steady.

5. Can I use inches of mercury, millibars or feet?

Yes. 📈 Trend › Units: hPa, mbar, inHg, mmHg or kPa, and metres or feet. The app starts with the usual units where your phone is set.

6. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
