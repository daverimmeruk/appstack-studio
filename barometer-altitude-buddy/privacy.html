Barometer & Altitude Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.barometerbuddy | Effective Date: 28 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Barometer & Altitude Buddy reads the air pressure with your phone's own pressure sensor. Everything stays on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic information, see section 4.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Pressure log: the readings saved every 5 minutes while the app is measuring (the time and the pressure), at most the last 31 days.
  • Height: the pressure and time where you last set zero, and the height of one floor.
  • Settings: the pressure unit, metres or feet, the height above sea level you typed in (if any), whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many useful results you have had (used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating).

3. Permissions Used

  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No other permission: no location, camera, microphone, storage, contacts, phone, SMS or notifications. The pressure sensor needs no permission, and the app reads it only while it's open and measuring (▶️ Start to ⏹ Stop).

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a measurement started, a zero set or a report made), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

5. Sharing

Something only leaves your phone when you choose to share it: the pressure report or the spreadsheet (Pro), or the manual.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • 📈 Trend › 🗑 Clear the log deletes all saved readings.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Barometer & Altitude Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

7. Children

Barometer & Altitude Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
