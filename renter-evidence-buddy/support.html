Renter Evidence Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. How do I start?

On the 🏠 Issues tab, tap ＋ New issue for each problem. Open it and add 📷 photos, 🌡️ readings and 📝 notes. Every time you report it, tap 📞 Log a report. When you need to send it, use 📄 Report › Share evidence PDF.

2. Why does a photo say "photo had no date"?

Some photos (for example screenshots or pictures sent in a chat) don't store the date they were taken. The app then uses the time you added the photo and says so. For the clearest record, use 📷 Take a photo now, or pick the original photo from your camera.

3. Why can't the app measure the temperature or humidity?

Phones have no humidity sensor, and the phone's own temperature isn't the room's. Type in the numbers from your own thermometer or humidity meter; the app works out the dew point from them.

4. What does the dew point mean?

It's the temperature at which water comes out of the air. Walls, windows or ceilings colder than the dew point get condensation on them. If you add a surface temperature (from an infrared thermometer), the app says whether that surface is at or below the dew point.

5. The photo reminder didn't come.

Reminders are part of Pro. Check that notifications are allowed for the app (Android asks when you switch a reminder on), and that the issue isn't marked fixed. Android runs the check about once a day, not at an exact time.

6. Will the council or a court accept it?

That is up to them. Advisers ask tenants to keep dated photos, notes and a record of every report; this app keeps them together and dated. For advice, contact Shelter, Citizens Advice or your council's private housing team.

7. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase. The record itself stays on the old phone: share the evidence PDF and the photos ZIP from it first.

8. What does the app do with my photos?

Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it yourself. See the Privacy Policy.
