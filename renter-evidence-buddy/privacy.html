Renter Evidence Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.renterevidencebuddy | Effective Date: 18 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Renter Evidence Buddy helps tenants keep a dated record of repairs their home needs, such as damp and mould, leaks or overheating. Your record, photos and signatures stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic logs, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Issues: the title, type, room, the date you noticed it, whether it is fixed, and the photo reminder you chose.
  • Photos: copies of the photos you take with the phone's camera app or pick with Android's photo picker, with the date stored in each photo (when it has one) and the time you added it.
  • Readings and notes: the temperatures and humidity you type in from your own meter, the dew point worked out from them, and your notes, each with its date and time.
  • Contacts: what you log about each report or reply: when, how, to or from whom, what was said, any reference number, and photos you attach.
  • Move-in and move-out checks (Pro): the rooms, conditions, notes, photos, meter readings, keys, and the names and on-screen signatures of the tenant and the landlord or agent, with the time each signed.
  • Settings: your details for the reports (name, address, landlord or agent, tenancy start date), the overheating limit, whether the welcome guide was shown and whether Pro is unlocked.

This information is kept in the app's own storage, which other apps can't read.

3. Camera and Photos

The app doesn't ask for camera or storage permission. When you tap 📷 Take a photo now, the phone's own camera app takes the photo and gives it to Renter Evidence Buddy. When you tap 🖼️ Choose from your photos, Android's photo picker gives the app only the photos you pick. The app reads the date stored in each photo; it doesn't use the photo's location.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the photo reminders you switch on (Pro). Android asks you first.
  • Background reminder check (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK, FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by Android's WorkManager library, which runs the once-a-day photo reminder check and keeps it after the phone restarts. The check only looks at the dates of your own photos on the phone.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

Android also lists internet permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the app only because Google Play's billing library, used for the Pro purchase, needs them; Renter Evidence Buddy itself sends nothing over the internet. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log or files, and uses no overlay, no accessibility service and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about connection problems, which Google uses to improve the library and help with errors. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example photos added to an issue, or a shared evidence pack), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. Reports, Photos and Manual

Your record only leaves your phone when you tap 📄 Share evidence PDF, 📄 Share PDF (move-in or move-out check), 🗂️ Share all photos full size (ZIP, Pro) or 📤 Share the manual, and share the file. It is then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a photo, reading or note with 🗑️ Delete on it; delete an issue with all its photos, readings and notes on the issue's page; delete a contact or a move check on its card or page.
  • 📄 Report › ⚙️ Settings › 🗑️ Delete everything deletes all of it.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Renter Evidence Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Renter Evidence Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone. Share a report first if you need to keep it.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Renter Evidence Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
