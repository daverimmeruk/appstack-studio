CellCoverage 5g & WaveScan Pro — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.cellcoverage5g | Effective Date: 9 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

CellCoverage 5g (WaveScan Pro) measures your own phone's mobile and Wi-Fi signal and shows it as live readings, a coverage map and room-by-room comparisons. Everything the app records is stored on your phone only. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics.

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Signal readings: your carrier name and network code, the network type (4G/5G), band and frequency, signal levels (RSRP, RSRQ, RSSI, SINR), cell identifiers (cell ID, PCI, TAC), the network's distance measurement when your phone reports it, and the GPS position and time of each reading. These draw your Map and 5G Heatmap.
  • Room readings (📶 Coverage and 📐 Blueprint): the signal levels you record in each room and the room names you type.
  • App settings: for example whether Pro is unlocked.

3. Permissions Used

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Android requires location permission before an app can read cell details. The app also uses your position to place your readings on the map and for the Blueprint's "walk to that room" warning. Location is used only while the app is open, never in the background.
  • Wi-Fi state (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): to read your Wi-Fi signal level for 📶 Coverage and 📐 Blueprint.
  • Internet and network state: to download map images and to check purchases with Google Play.

The app does not use the phone permission, your contacts, camera or microphone.

4. Third-Party Services

  • OpenStreetMap: map images (tiles) are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address, the app's name and which map area is being loaded. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Prices and any free trial are shown by Google Play at checkout.
  • Android location services: your position comes from your phone's location service (Google Play services), which follows your phone's location settings and Google's Privacy Policy.

5. PDF Reports (Pro)

A PDF report is created on your phone only when you tap Export. It is shared only with the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Your readings stay on your phone until you delete them. In 📐 Blueprint, long-press a room tab to clear that room's readings or delete the room. Map readings are deleted by clearing the app's storage (below).
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › CellCoverage 5g › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • If Android backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own backup. This is controlled by your phone's settings.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it stores or shares, we will update this page and its effective date.
