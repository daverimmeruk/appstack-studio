CellCoverage 5g & WaveScan Pro — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.cellcoverage.cellcoverage5g | Effective Date: October 2026 | Developer: Appstack-Studio

1. Overview & Data Philosophy

Appstack-Studio built CellCoverage 5g (WaveScan Pro) as an independent 5G/4G cellular telemetry and tower directional locator application. We prioritize your privacy: all personal data, location history, and cellular signal logs are kept 100% on your device and are never transmitted, sold, or shared with external ad networks or data brokers.

2. Required Technical Permissions & Usage

  • Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Used strictly on your device to calculate real-time 360° azimuth bearing angles, distance in meters, line-of-sight elevation, and render OpenStreetMap vector map layers. Location data is processed locally on your phone.
  • Telephony & Network Signaling (READ_PHONE_STATE): Used exclusively on-device via Android's native TelephonyManager hardware APIs to read 3GPP signal metrics (RSRP dBm, RSRQ dB, SINR, frequency bands, Cell IDs, PCI, TAC, and MCC-MNC operator codes).
  • In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing API): Handled securely by Google Play Console to process optional 3-Day Free Trial subscriptions for WaveScan Pro features.

3. Third-Party Open Source APIs

The application integrates open-source, privacy-focused services:

  • OpenStreetMap (osmdroid) & Nominatim API: Used for worldwide vector map rendering and universal city/zip location search.
  • Postcodes.io API: Used for open-source UK postcode lookup.

4. Local Data Storage & User Control

All signal telemetry logs and cached searches are stored locally in an encrypted Room SQLite database on your device. You can clear or export your logs at any time via the Share Report feature.
