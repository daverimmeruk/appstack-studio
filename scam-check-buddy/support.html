Scam Check Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. It says "No warning signs found". Is the message safe?

Not necessarily. Scam Check Buddy lists the signs it finds, and a new kind of scam may show none. If a message asks for money, details or a code, check with the sender another way first.

2. Why does a genuine message show a warning sign?

Some genuine messages use the same words as scams (for example "verify your details"). The signs explain what was found so you can judge. If you're unsure, open the company's own app or type its website in yourself.

3. How do I check a text message?

  • Long-press the message, tap Copy, then open Scam Check Buddy and tap 📋 Paste.
  • Or select the text and pick Scam check from the menu (on some phones under ⋮ or "More").
  • Or use Share on the message, where your messages app offers it, and pick Scam Check Buddy.

4. Does checking a link online open the website?

No. The domain register is asked how old the website is, and a short-link service is asked where its link points. The website itself is never contacted, so it can't see that you checked it.

5. The QR scanner doesn't start.

The scanner comes from Google Play services. Check they are up to date in the Play Store, and be online the first time you use it so Android can download it.

6. I've used my 3 free online checks.

They come back tomorrow (from midnight on your phone). Pro checks links online as often as you like. The check on the phone itself is always free and unlimited.

7. I think I've been scammed. What now?

Call your bank straight away, on the number on your card or 159, and report it to the police. Forward scam texts to 7726 and scam emails to report@phishing.gov.uk.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

9. Can Scam Check Buddy read my texts?

No. It has no SMS permission and only sees what you paste, share or select. See the Privacy Policy.
