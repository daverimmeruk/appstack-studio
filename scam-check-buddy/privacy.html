Scam Check Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.scamcheckbuddy | Effective Date: 19 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Scam Check Buddy checks a message, link or QR code you're not sure about and lists the warning signs it finds. The messages you check stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. (Google's QR scanner sends Google its own diagnostic data, see section 6.) The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics.

2. What the App Sees

  • Only the text you paste into it, share to it, or select in another app and send with "Scam check", and the text in a QR code you scan.
  • Scam Check Buddy can't read your texts, emails, chats, contacts or call log by itself. It has no SMS, contacts or call-log permission.
  • The message check itself runs on your phone, with no internet.

3. The Online Link Check (only when you tap it)

  • Website age: the domain name of each web address (for example example.top, never the rest of the address or your message) is sent to the official domain register for that ending, found in IANA's public list (for example Verisign for .com or Nominet for .uk), which answers with the registration date. The register sees your internet (IP) address, as with any connection.
  • Short links: a short link (for example from bit.ly or tinyurl.com) is sent to the short-link service it belongs to, which answers with where it points. Scam Check Buddy reads that answer but doesn't follow it, so the website at the end is never contacted.
  • Nothing else is sent, and we receive nothing.

4. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Saved checks (Pro): the message you checked (up to 3,000 characters), the date and time, and what was found.
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many free online checks you used today.

Scam Check Buddy keeps up to 200 saved checks; when full, the oldest is removed first.

5. Permissions Used

  • Internet (INTERNET): for the online link check described above, and used by Google Play's billing library for the Pro purchase.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to tell you when there is no internet before an online check, and used by Google Play's billing library.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

The QR scanner is Google's code scanner, which runs in Google Play services and shows the camera itself, so Scam Check Buddy doesn't need the camera permission and never receives camera pictures, only the scanned text. The app does not use your location, contacts, SMS, call log, microphone, photos or files, and uses no overlay and no accessibility service.

6. Third-Party Services

  • Domain registers and short-link services: see section 3.
  • Google Play services code scanner (ML Kit): see section 5. When you use the QR scanner, Google's ML Kit sends Google diagnostic and usage data: device information (maker, model, Android version), app name and version, a per-installation identifier that is not meant to identify you or your phone, and performance and error figures. It is encrypted in transit, used by Google for diagnostics and usage analytics, and not shared with third parties. It never includes your messages or the scanned code. See ML Kit's data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about connection problems, which Google uses to improve the library and help with errors. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few checks the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

7. PDF and Manual

A check only leaves your phone when you tap Share as PDF (Pro) or Share the manual and share the file. It is then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

8. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete one saved check from its details, or all with Results › Clear all.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Scam Check Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Scam Check Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

9. Children

Scam Check Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
