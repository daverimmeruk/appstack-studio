📖 Car Finder — Comprehensive Field Guide

Complete step-by-step instructions and why Home Guardian outperforms expensive rivals.

🛡️ PART 1: Home Anti-Theft Guardian & Setup (FREE)

Protect your vehicle parked on your driveway without any extra hardware:

  • Step 1: Pair your car Bluetooth in Android Settings for the first time.
  • Step 2: Open Car Finder, tap 'Scan & Select Home Wi-Fi' to select your home network, and tap 'Find & Connect Car Bluetooth' to select your car from your paired list, then tap 'Save Settings'.
  • Step 3 (Arming): Once parked at home with both Wi-Fi & Bluetooth connected (Green light 🟢), toggle 'Enable Home Guardian' ON.
  • Step 4 (Test Alarm & Audio Modes): Tap 'Test Alarm' anytime to test the full-screen red warning banner. On the alarm screen, tap the audio mode button to toggle live between 'Sound Only', 'Voice Speech Only', or 'Sound & Voice'.
  • Step 5 (Custom Alarm Sound): Tap '🎵 Choose Custom Alarm Sound' to pick any custom warning sound from your phone.
  • Step 6 (Export & Import Credentials): Back up or restore your setup string instantly (with auto-clipboard detection for imports).

🗺️ PART 2: Outside Parking & Map AR Compass (PRO)

Mark your car's location when parking outside and navigate back:

  • 1. When parking in open-air lots or streets, open the Outside Map & AR tab and tap '📍 Mark Parked Car Here' to lock in your coordinates.
  • 2. Watch the extra-large 32sp distance overlay (e.g. 🚗 12m) right on the OpenStreetMap view when returning.
  • 3. Rotate your phone until crosshairs turn Bright Neon Green (🟢 100% LINE OF SIGHT ACQUIRED) pointing straight to your vehicle.

📡 PART 3: 🅿️ UNDERGROUND Parking Radar (PRO)

Find your way back in multi-story or underground car parks:

  • 1. When parked in an underground or multi-story bay, open the Underground Radar tab and tap '📍 Mark Parked Car Here' right on the radar screen to lock in your position.
  • 2. Tap 'Start Radar Scan' to measure signal strength and detect parking floor levels (-1, Basement, Street Level).
  • 3. Follow the large rotating compass arrow pointer and directional turn instructions (e.g., 'Turn Right 45°').
  • 4. Tap '🔄 Refresh' anytime to reset the radar scanner.
