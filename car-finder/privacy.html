Your Privacy is 100% Protected

At AppStack Studio, we respect your privacy. **Car Finder & Anti-Theft Guardian** processes all location data (GPS coordinates, Wi-Fi SSID, and Bluetooth connection state) **strictly and exclusively on-device**.

Data Collection & Usage

  • Location Data: GPS coordinates are stored locally on your device solely to help you navigate back to your parked car. They are never uploaded to external servers.
  • Wi-Fi & Bluetooth States: Monitored locally on-device in real-time to power the Home Anti-Theft Guardian feature. Credentials are encrypted locally and never shared.
  • Third Parties: We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data or location metrics with third parties.
