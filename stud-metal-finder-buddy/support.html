Stud & Metal Finder Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Can it find wooden studs?

Not the wood itself: it finds the screws and nails that hold the plasterboard to the studs. A row of fixings going up the wall marks a stud; the next is usually 400 mm or 600 mm along.

2. Can it find cables or water pipes?

No. Copper wire and copper or plastic pipes aren't magnetic, so the app can't see them. Always check with a proper cable and pipe detector before drilling, and keep out of the cable zones (see 📖 Manual).

3. The reading keeps wandering.

Unplug the charger, take the phone out of any magnetic case, hold it flat on the wall away from screws and tap 🎯 Zero. Don't turn the phone while you search: turning it changes the reading too.

4. Where is the sensor in my phone?

Hold a screw or key against the back of the phone and move it around: the reading jumps most over the sensor, often near the top.

5. How deep does it see?

About 2–3 cm into a wall for a screw or nail; bigger steel (like a lintel) shows from further away.

6. The reading is high (or it beeps) all the time.

Tap 🔄 Reset with the phone flat on the wall, away from screws: a Zero taken in the air or in your hand makes the reading high everywhere on the wall, because tilting the phone changes the reading. If it's still high, tap 🎚️ for a lower sensitivity. Beeps are off until you switch them on.

7. How do I stop it?

Tap ⏹️ Stop: the reading goes back to 0.0 and nothing is read until you tap 🎯 Zero again. It also stops when you leave the app.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
