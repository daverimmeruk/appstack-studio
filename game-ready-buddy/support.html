Game Ready Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. My ping in a game is different from Game Ready Buddy's. Why?

Game Ready Buddy measures the round trip to the test server you pick (Cloudflare or Google), which answers from its nearest site. A game's own server can be in another city or country, so the ping inside a game can be higher. Jitter, packet loss and lag spikes on your own Wi-Fi or mobile connection affect every game, so they are the numbers to watch.

2. What is a good result?

The grade limits are in the Manual tab under "Report tab and grades". For example ping up to 60 ms, jitter up to 15 ms and packet loss up to 1% are Good for fast online games.

3. My connection is graded Fair or Poor. What can I do?

  • Play closer to your router, or move the router somewhere more open.
  • Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi if your router offers it (the card on the Ping tab shows the band).
  • Stop downloads, video and updates on other devices while you play.
  • With Pro, run Wi-Fi vs mobile data to see which is better right now.

4. Wi-Fi vs mobile data says mobile data couldn't be tested.

  • Mobile data must be switched on and the phone needs a SIM with a data allowance.
  • If the result says Android wants a sign-in or top-up page, your mobile network is blocking the internet (for example no credit left).
  • Some networks can't reach the test server's IPv4 address at first; Game Ready Buddy then uses IPv6 and says "over IPv6".

5. Why is my touch sample rate lower than my phone's advertised rate?

Some phones only use their fastest touch rate inside games, for example through a game booster or game mode. Game Ready Buddy shows the rate the phone gives to an ordinary app. Keep one finger moving steadily for a few seconds for the best reading.

6. The screen test shows 60 frames per second on a 120 Hz phone.

  • Check Settings › Display (Motion smoothness or Smooth display) is set to the high or adaptive rate.
  • Power saving often limits the screen to 60 Hz: the card on the Screen tab warns you when it's on.

7. The heat test stopped early.

For your phone's safety it stops by itself if the battery reaches 46 °C or Android reports that the phone is very hot. It also stops if you leave the app. Let the phone cool down, take it out of a thick case, unplug the charger and try again.

8. Does Game Ready Buddy make my phone faster?

No. It doesn't change any settings and doesn't run in the background. It measures, so you can see where the problem is and what to change.

9. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

10. Where are my results kept?

On your phone only. They aren't backed up or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
