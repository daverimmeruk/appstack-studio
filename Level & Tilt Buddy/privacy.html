Level & Tilt Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.leveltiltbuddy | Effective Date: 17 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Level & Tilt Buddy is a spirit level and angle meter. Your settings and readings stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic logs, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Calibration: how far off your phone's sensor was, for lying flat and for each way of standing it up (landscape and portrait).
  • Settings: your choices in ⚙️ Settings (units, level limit, beep and buzz), a "Zero" surface if you set one (Pro), whether the welcome guide was shown and whether Pro is unlocked.
  • Caravan & motorhome (Pro): the vehicle type, the measurements and ramp steps you type in, and which way the phone points.
  • Saved readings (Pro): the name you type, the date and time, the reading, and whether the phone was calibrated.

This information is kept in the app's own storage, which other apps can't read.

3. Sensors

The app reads the phone's motion sensors (the accelerometer for the angles, and the gyroscope to check the phone was turned round while calibrating) only while the app is open on the screen. These readings are used on the phone and are not stored or sent anywhere; only the calibration results above are kept.

4. Permissions Used

  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

Motion sensors need no permission. Android also lists internet permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the app only because Google Play's billing library, used for the Pro purchase, needs them; Level & Tilt Buddy itself sends nothing over the internet. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, call log, photos, files or notifications, and uses no overlay, no accessibility service and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about connection problems, which Google uses to improve the library and help with errors. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a finished calibration or a saved reading), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. PDF, CSV and Manual

Your saved readings only leave your phone when you tap Share PDF or Share CSV (Pro), or Share the manual, and share the file. It is then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a saved reading by tapping it on the 💾 Saved tab, then Delete. ⚙️ Settings › Clear calibration removes the calibration.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Level & Tilt Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Level & Tilt Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone (a calibration belongs to one phone's sensor).
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Level & Tilt Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
