Level & Tilt Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. The reading isn't 0 on a surface I know is level.

Calibrate: 🎯 Calibrate, Step 1, spin the phone round on the same spot (half a turn), Step 2. Do it lying flat and again for each way you stand it up (landscape or portrait), and again after changing the phone's case. The surface doesn't have to be level for this.

2. Calibration says the phone hasn't been turned round.

Between Step 1 and Step 2, spin the phone half a turn on the same spot: lying flat, like a turntable, so its top points the other way; standing up, keep the same side on the surface and spin it like a revolving door, so the screen faces the other way (don't turn it over onto its other side). On phones with a gyroscope the app checks this.

3. The numbers keep changing.

Put the phone on a firm surface and don't touch it. Wait for 🟢 Steady. Use ⏸ Hold to freeze a reading, or the beep and buzz to know when it's level without touching the phone.

4. Which way is the bubble?

Like a real spirit level, the bubble goes to the HIGH side. Under the numbers it says which side or end is low, for example "Right side low".

5. How do I measure upright, like on a door frame?

Stand the phone up against the surface: portrait (vertical) for a door frame, or landscape (horizontal) on its long side for a shelf. The tube bubble appears and the picture turns so it's the right way up for you. Leaning the phone forwards or backwards doesn't change the reading.

6. How do I level my caravan or motorhome?

With Pro, open 🚐 Caravan, tap ✏️ Enter your measurements (width between the wheels, jockey wheel to axle, or front to back axle for a motorhome, and your ramp steps), then lay the phone flat on the floor with its top pointing to the front. It shows which wheel to raise and by how many cm.

7. Can I switch the beep off?

Yes: ⚙️ Settings › Beep and buzz when level.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

9. Does Level & Tilt Buddy need any permissions?

It never asks you for any. It reads the motion sensors, which need no permission, and keeps everything on your phone. (Google Play's billing, used for the Pro purchase, adds its own internet permission.) See the Privacy Policy.
