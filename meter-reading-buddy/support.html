Meter Reading Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Which numbers do I type?

The numbers on the meter, left to right. On most electricity and gas meters, ignore red digits and anything after a decimal point. On a smart meter's display, use the reading your supplier asks for (often labelled 1.8.0 or IMP).

2. Why a photo?

A dated photo of the meter shows your reading was right. Send it with the PDF if a bill is estimated or doesn't match your meter.

3. My gas meter shows cubic feet.

When you add the gas meter, choose "Hundreds of cubic feet". The app converts to m³ and kWh for you.

4. Why doesn't the cost match my bill exactly?

The estimate uses your unit price and standing charge, and a typical gas calorific value (39.5). Your bill uses the exact calorific value and may include price changes, discounts or VAT differences.

5. It says my reading is lower than last time.

Meters don't go backwards unless the meter was replaced. Check the digits; if the meter was changed, add the new meter as a new one.

6. I didn't get the reminder.

Check the reminder day in 📄 Reports. If Android blocks the app's notifications, 📄 Reports shows 🔕 Notifications are blocked: tap it to allow them. Android may show the reminder a little after 09:00.

7. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
