Home Inventory Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.homeinventorybuddy | Effective Date: 27 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Home Inventory Buddy keeps a list of the things you own, with photos and receipts. Your list, photos and details stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic logs, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Your inventory: rooms, and for each item the name, type, value, quantity, make or model, serial number, shop, purchase date, warranty date and notes you type in.
  • Photos and receipts: the pictures you take or choose for each item, stored as smaller copies (at most 1600 pixels across) in the app's own folder. The date stored in a photo is kept.
  • Your details (optional): name, address and policy number, only printed on your PDFs.
  • Settings: warranty reminders on or off, which warranty reminders were already shown, when you last made a backup, your currency if you chose one, whether the welcome guide was shown and whether Pro is unlocked.

This information is kept in the app's own storage, which other apps can't read, and it is not included in Android's own backups.

3. Photos

Photos come from your phone's own camera app or from Android's photo picker, which only gives the app the pictures you choose. The app has no camera or storage permission and never reads your other photos.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only for the warranty reminders, if you switch them on (Pro). Android asks you first.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

Android also lists internet permissions (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) because Google Play's billing library needs them, and permissions used by Android's WorkManager to run the once-a-day reminder check (WAKE_LOCK, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, FOREGROUND_SERVICE). Home Inventory Buddy itself sends nothing over the internet. It does not use your location, contacts, microphone, SMS or call log, and uses no overlay, no accessibility service and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about connection problems, which Google uses to improve the library and help with errors.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example an item saved with a photo, a PDF or a backup made), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. PDFs, Spreadsheet, Backup and Manual

Your list only leaves your phone when you share a PDF, the spreadsheet (Pro), a backup ZIP (Pro) or the manual. It is then sent only to the app, place or person you choose on Android's share screen, for example your own Google Drive or email. A backup ZIP contains your whole list and every photo, so keep it somewhere private.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete an item (🗑 Delete) or a room with everything in it (🗑 Delete room). A photo or receipt can be deleted by tapping it.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Home Inventory Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. Backup ZIPs you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Home Inventory Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
