Home Inventory Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Where is my list kept?

Only on your phone, in the app's own storage. Make a backup (📄 Reports › 💾 Make a backup, Pro) and save it somewhere else, for example your Google Drive or your email, so the list survives if the phone is stolen or broken.

2. How do I move my list to a new phone?

On the old phone, make a backup ZIP and save it to Google Drive (or email it to yourself). On the new phone, install Home Inventory Buddy, open 📄 Reports › ♻️ Restore from a backup and choose the ZIP. Restoring is free.

3. Restore says the file isn't a backup.

Choose the ZIP made by Home Inventory Buddy (its name starts with home_inventory_backup). Nothing in the app is changed when a file can't be used.

4. What value should I enter?

What it would cost to replace the item today, or what you paid. Check your policy: some pay "new for old", some take off wear and tear. Your insurer decides what a claim pays.

5. Does the app send my list to my insurer?

No. Home Inventory Buddy doesn't sell or arrange insurance and doesn't contact any insurer. You decide who gets the PDF.

6. Where do I find a serial number?

On a label on the back or underneath (TVs, laptops, appliances), in the box or the manual, or under the pedals on a bike frame. Take a photo of the label too.

7. I didn't get a warranty reminder.

Check that 🔔 Warranty reminders is on in 📄 Reports (Pro), that notifications are allowed for the app, and that the item has a warranty date. Android runs the check about once a day, not at an exact time; each warranty reminds you once, 30 days before it ends (again if you change its date). If Android blocks the app's notifications, 📄 Reports shows 🔕 Notifications are blocked: tap it to allow them.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

9. Does Home Inventory Buddy need any permissions?

Only notifications, and only if you switch on warranty reminders. Photos come from your camera app or Android's photo picker, with no camera or storage permission. See the Privacy Policy.
