👋 What Home Inventory Buddy does

Home Inventory Buddy keeps a list of the things you own, room by room, with photos, what each one is worth, when and where you bought it, serial numbers, warranties and receipts. After a burglary, fire or flood, insurers ask what you had and what it was worth: the PDF list answers that, with the photos as proof.

Home Inventory Buddy is a list of your belongings. It doesn't sell or arrange insurance, and it doesn't contact your insurer: you decide who to send the PDF to.

🏠 Rooms

Start with 🏠 Rooms › ＋ Add a room, and pick a room (Living room, Kitchen, Garage…) or type your own. Each room shows how many items it has and what they're worth. Tap a room to see its items; ✏️ renames it, 🗑 deletes it with everything in it.

📦 Adding an item

In a room, tap ＋ Add an item. Only the name is needed; the rest helps a claim:

  • Type (electronics, furniture, jewellery…)
  • Value: what it would cost to replace (or what you paid), for one, and how many you have
  • Make / model and serial number (on a label on the back or underneath; police use it to return stolen goods)
  • When and where you bought it, and when the warranty ends
  • Notes

Then add 📷 photos of the item and 🧾 receipts: take a photo now, or choose ones you already have. Photos are stored at up to 1600 pixels across, which is plenty for a claim and saves space.

💡 Tips for a good inventory

  • Photograph the whole room first, then each valuable thing.
  • Photograph serial numbers and labels.
  • Jewellery, watches and art: add close-up photos and any valuation certificate as a receipt.
  • Do one room at a time; it's quicker than it sounds.
  • Update it when you buy something expensive, and make a new backup.

🔎 Items

The 🔎 Items tab lists everything in every room, with a search box. Tap an item to see or change it. ⏳ Warranty ending shows items whose warranty ends in the next 30 days.

📄 Reports

  • 📄 Inventory PDF: every room with a table of totals, then each item with its first photo and details (free).
  • 🧾 Claim pack (Pro): tick the items you are claiming for, for example everything stolen from the living room. The PDF lists them with their total, then every photo and receipt of each item, large.
  • 📊 Spreadsheet (Pro): every item as a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets.

Your name, address and policy number (✏️ Your details) are printed at the top of the PDFs if you enter them. They stay on this phone.

💾 Backup and restore

Your list is kept only on this phone. If the phone is stolen in the same burglary, or breaks, the list goes with it, so keep a backup somewhere else.

  • 💾 Make a backup (Pro) makes one ZIP file with the list and every photo. Save it to Google Drive or another cloud you use, email it to yourself, or copy it to a computer. Make a new one after adding things.
  • ♻️ Restore from a backup (free) reads a backup ZIP, for example on a new phone, and replaces what is in the app with it. It asks first, and checks the file before changing anything.

🔔 Warranty reminders (Pro)

Switch on 🔔 Warranty reminders in 📄 Reports. About once a day Android lets the app check, and for each item whose warranty ends in the next 30 days you get one notification, so there's still time to get a fault repaired. If you change the warranty date, you're reminded again for the new date. Android may ask you to allow notifications.

If Android blocks the app's notifications, 📄 Reports shows 🔕 Notifications are blocked: tap it to allow them.

⭐ Free and Pro

Free: up to 2 rooms and 25 items, with photos, receipts, values and totals, the inventory PDF, and restoring a backup. No ads.

Pro (one payment, no subscription): unlimited rooms and items, the backup ZIP, the claim pack, the spreadsheet and warranty reminders.

Tap GO PRO at the top. Restore purchase gets Pro back on a new phone with the same Google account.

🔒 Privacy

No account, no sign-in, and no ads. Your list, photos and details are kept only in the app's own storage on this phone, and they are not included in Android's own backups. Home Inventory Buddy has no servers. A PDF, spreadsheet or backup only leaves the phone when you share it yourself.

Google Play handles the Pro purchase.
