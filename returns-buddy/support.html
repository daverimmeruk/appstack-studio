Returns Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. How many days do I have to return something?

It depends on the shop: check its returns policy. In the UK and the EU you can usually cancel most online orders within 14 days of delivery; in other countries it depends on the shop's own policy. Many shops give 30 days or more. Tap 14, 30 or 60 days, or pick the exact date.

2. When will I be reminded?

At about 09:00, 3 days and 1 day before the deadline, and on the day. After you send something back, 14 and 30 days later if no refund has been marked. Check that reminders are on in 📄 Reports. If Android blocks the app's notifications, 📄 Reports shows 🔕 Notifications are blocked: tap it to allow them.

3. What photos should I take?

The receipt or order email, the item (especially any damage), the parcel once packed, and the drop-off or postage receipt. Each photo keeps the time it was added.

4. How do I send proof to the shop?

Tap the return, then 📄 Proof PDF, and choose your email app. The PDF has all the details and every photo.

5. The refund was less than I paid.

Type the amount you got back: the app shows the difference on the return and in ✅ Done.

6. Does it read my emails or track parcels?

No. The app doesn't connect to email, shops or couriers. The details are the ones you type in.

7. Can I use another currency?

Yes. Prices and refunds use your phone's currency, written the way your phone writes money. To use another one, tap Change the currency in 📄 Reports. The app never converts amounts: the numbers you typed stay as they are.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
