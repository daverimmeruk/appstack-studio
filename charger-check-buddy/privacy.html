Charger Check Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.chargercheckbuddy | Effective Date: 19 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Charger Check Buddy measures how much current goes into your battery from each charger and cable, estimates your battery's health, and lists the apps that keep running in the background. Everything the app saves is stored on your phone only. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, has no ads and no analytics, and sends nothing over the internet itself.

2. What the App Reads on Your Phone

  • Battery readings: the current going into or out of the battery, the charge in the battery, battery level, voltage, temperature, health status, technology, charge cycles (where the phone reports them) and what the phone is plugged into. These come from Android's battery service.
  • Rated battery capacity: read from the phone's own power profile.
  • Phone details: model, Android version and security update, chip, processor cores, memory, storage and screen.
  • Usage record (only if you switch on Usage access): which apps kept a background service running, when the screen was on or off, and how many new notifications apps showed. It is read on your phone to make the always-running list and the drain test result, and never leaves your phone.

3. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Charger tests: the name you gave the charger, the date and time, the measured current and power, the battery level and temperature, and the grade.
  • Drain tests: the start time and the battery's charge at the start, then the result: test time, charge used, drain per hour, screen-off and screen-on time and the apps that ran in the background.
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and whether this phone reports current in µA or mA.

Charger Check Buddy keeps up to 200 results; when full, the oldest is removed first.

4. Permissions and Access Used

  • Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): only if you switch it on in Android's settings, to list the apps that kept running in the background. You can switch it off at any time.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

Android lists an internet permission (INTERNET) and a network-state permission (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) for the app only because Google Play's billing library, used for the Pro purchase, needs them; Charger Check Buddy itself sends nothing over the internet. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, photos, files or the phone permission, and uses no overlay and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about connection problems, which Google uses to improve the library and help with errors. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few finished tests the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. Copy, PDF Report and Manual

Your details and results only leave your phone when you choose to: 📋 Copy puts the battery and phone details on your clipboard for you to paste, and PDF report (Pro) or Share the manual makes a file that is sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete one result from its details (tap a result), or all results with Results › Clear all.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Charger Check Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Charger Check Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone, so it stays on this phone only.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Charger Check Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it stores or shares, we will update this page and its effective date.
