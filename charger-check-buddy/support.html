Charger Check Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Why is the charging current lower than my charger's label?

The label says the most the charger can give. The phone decides how much it takes, and Charger Check Buddy measures what goes into the battery: the phone itself uses some, so the charger always gives a little more. Phones also take less when the battery is above about 80% or warm.

2. My charger test says "no grade". Why?

  • The battery was full or nearly full, so the phone took very little charge.
  • The phone was plugged in but not charging, for example because battery protection stops charging at 80% or 85%.
  • Test again below 80% for a result that can grade the charger.

3. How do I find out if a cable is bad?

Test two cables with the same plug, one after the other, at about the same battery level. If one gives much less current, that cable is slowing your charging down. With Pro, the Results tab ranks every test fastest first.

4. Why does my battery health show over 100%?

The rated capacity in the phone's power profile is usually the maker's minimum, so a new battery can hold a little more. The estimate is most exact at 95% or more, so charge to 100% and look again.

5. The health estimate says "not available".

  • It needs the battery at 50% or more.
  • A few phones don't give apps the rated capacity or the charge in the battery; the app then says so.

6. Why can't the app show my battery's serial number or manufacture date?

Android keeps these, and its own health percentage, for its system apps only. No app from Google Play can read them. Some phones show more in their own Settings app.

7. The always-running list is empty or asks for Usage access.

Tap Allow usage access, find Charger Check Buddy and switch it on. Android then shares its usage record with the app; it stays on your phone.

8. An app runs in the background all the time. What can I do?

Tap it in the list to open its settings. The Battery option there lets you limit its background use. Some apps need to run (for example a fitness tracker or an automation app), so only limit the ones you don't need.

9. My drain test says it can't be measured.

  • It needs at least 1 hour.
  • The phone must be unplugged when you finish, and must not have been charged during the test.

10. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.

11. Where are my results kept?

On your phone only. They aren't backed up or sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy.
