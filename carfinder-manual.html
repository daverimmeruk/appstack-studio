📖 Complete Step-by-Step User Manual & Field Guide

1. Home Anti-Theft Guardian & Audio Modes (FREE)

Protect your vehicle parked on your driveway without any extra hardware:

  • Step 1: Tap '🔍 Scan & Select Home Wi-Fi' to select your home network SSID.
  • Step 2: Tap '🔍 Find & Connect Car Bluetooth' or the How-To guide. Most cars 2018+ have built-in Bluetooth (older cars use an adapter). Pair your car audio in Android settings. Any Bluetooth connection type works (handsfree calls or media audio). If unsure, refer to your car's user manual.
  • Step 3: Tap 'Save Settings', then toggle 'Enable Home Guardian' to ON.
  • Step 4 (Test Alarm & Audio Modes): Tap 'Test Alarm' anytime to test the full-screen red warning banner. On the alarm screen, tap the audio mode button to toggle live between 'Sound Only', 'Voice Speech Only', or 'Sound & Voice'.
  • Step 5 (Custom Alarm Sound): Tap '🎵 Choose Custom Alarm Sound' to pick any custom warning sound from your phone.
  • Step 6 (Export & Import Credentials): Back up or restore your setup string instantly (with auto-clipboard detection for imports).

2. Outside Parking & Map AR Compass (PRO)

Mark your car's location when parking outside and navigate back:

  • Step 1: When parking in open-air lots or streets, open the Outside Map & AR tab and tap '📍 Mark Parked Car Here' to lock in your coordinates.
  • Step 2: Watch the extra-large 32sp distance overlay (e.g. 🚗 12m) right on the OpenStreetMap view when returning.
  • Step 3: Rotate your phone until crosshairs turn Bright Neon Green (🟢 100% LINE OF SIGHT ACQUIRED) pointing straight to your vehicle.

3. 🅿️ UNDERGROUND Parking Radar (PRO)

Find your way back in multi-story or underground car parks:

  • Step 1: When parked in an underground or multi-story bay, open the Underground Radar tab and tap '📍 Mark Parked Car Here' right on the radar screen to lock in your position.
  • Step 2: Tap 'Start Radar Scan' to measure signal strength and detect parking floor levels (-1, Basement, Street Level).
  • Step 3: Follow the large rotating compass arrow pointer and directional turn instructions (e.g., 'Turn Right 45°').
  • Step 4: Tap '🔄 Refresh' anytime to reset the radar scanner.

4. PRO Suite & 3-Day Free Trial

Home Anti-Theft Guardian is 100% Free forever. Advanced features (Underground Radar & Outside Map AR) are part of the Pro Suite:

  • Start a 3-Day Free Trial anytime by tapping any Pro tab.
  • Upgrade to Lifetime Pro (£7.99) or Monthly Subscription (£2.99/mo).
