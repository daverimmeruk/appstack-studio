📖 Complete Step-by-Step User Manual & Field Guide

1. Home Anti-Theft Guardian (Free Edition)

Protect your vehicle parked on your driveway without any extra hardware:

  • Step 1: Open the Guardian tab and tap 'Scan & Select Home Wi-Fi' to select your home network SSID.
  • Step 2: Tap 'Find & Connect Car Bluetooth' to pair your car headunit (e.g. Android-Vlink).
  • Step 3: Tap 'Save Settings', then toggle 'Enable Home Guardian' to ON.
  • Step 4: If your car moves away while you are home, an emergency theft alarm triggers instantly with a voice warning and full-screen red alert!

2. Wi-Fi Parking Radar & Level Detector (PRO Suite)

Find your way back in multi-story or underground car parks where GPS is unavailable:

  • Step 1: Tap 'Start Radar Scan' to measure RSSI signal proximity and detect parking floor levels (-1, Basement, Street Level).
  • Step 2: Follow the large rotating compass arrow pointer and directional turn instructions (e.g., 'Turn Right 45°').
  • Step 3: Tap '🔄 Refresh' anytime to reset the radar scanner.

3. Map & AR Line-of-Sight Compass (PRO Suite)

Navigate directly to your parked car with visual crosshairs in open-air lots and streets:

  • Step 1: Tap 'Mark Parked Car Here' when parking.
  • Step 2: Watch the extra-large 32sp distance overlay (e.g. 🚗 12m) right on the OpenStreetMap view.
  • Step 3: Rotate your phone until crosshairs turn Bright Neon Green (🟢 100% LINE OF SIGHT ACQUIRED) pointing straight to your vehicle.

4. PRO Suite & 3-Day Free Trial

Home Guardian is 100% Free forever. Advanced features (Radar & AR Compass) are part of the Pro Suite:

  • Start a 3-Day Free Trial anytime by tapping any Pro tab.
  • Upgrade to Lifetime Pro (£7.99) or Monthly Subscription (£2.99/mo).
