Noise Diary Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. The night log saves too many entries (fridge, traffic, snoring).

Raise the trigger level: ⚙️ Settings › 🔔 Trigger level. You can also make it ignore short bumps with ⏱️ Count noise lasting at least (0.5, 1, 2 or 5 seconds).

2. Noise I can hear isn't saved.

Lower the trigger level. Look at the 🎚️ Meter when the noise happens and set the trigger a little below that number. Put the phone where you hear the noise, with nothing covering the microphone.

3. The night log stopped by itself during the night.

Some phones close apps to save battery. Keep the phone on charge, and in the phone's Settings › Apps › Noise Diary Buddy › Battery, choose Unrestricted. Don't play music or videos on the phone while it listens.

4. Is the dB number exact?

No. It is measured with the phone's microphone, which isn't a calibrated sound level meter, so it is approximate. If you can borrow a real sound level meter, use ⚙️ Settings › 🎯 Calibrate.

5. Can I turn the sound clips off?

Yes: ⚙️ Settings › 🎧 Sound clips › Off. The night log then keeps the times and levels only.

6. How do I give the diary to the council or my landlord?

Open 📄 Report, choose the layout and period, add your details if you like, and tap 📄 Share PDF to email, print or save it. With Pro you can also share the sound clips and photos as one ZIP file.

7. Will a court accept it?

That is up to them. A diary with dates, times, lengths, effects and witnesses is what they usually ask for; the levels and sound clips support it. Many councils also lend their own recording equipment.

8. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase. The diary itself stays on the old phone: share a report from it first.

9. What does Noise Diary Buddy do with my recordings?

Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it yourself. Clips are only recorded for noise at or above your trigger level, while a night log you started is running. See the Privacy Policy.
