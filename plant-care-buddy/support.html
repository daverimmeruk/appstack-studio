Plant Care Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. How often should I water?

It depends on the plant, the pot and the season. Many houseplants like water every 7 to 14 days, when the top few centimetres of soil are dry; herbs and small pots dry out faster, cacti much slower. Feel the soil and adjust the days.

2. The soil is still damp. What do I do?

Tap ⏭️ Tomorrow on 🌿 Today: the plant moves on by one day without counting as watered.

3. When will I be reminded?

At about 09:00 on days any plant needs water or feed: one note with their names. Check that reminders are on in 📄 Reports and that notifications are allowed.

4. How do I make a care sheet for a plant sitter?

On 📄 Reports, add their name or your dates away, then tap 📄 Care sheet and send it by email or message, or print it. It shows where each plant is, how often to water and feed, the next dates and a photo.

5. What do the light settings mean?

For indoor plants: low light is about 500–2,500 lux, medium light about 2,500–10,000 lux, high light about 10,000–25,000 lux and direct sun about 25,000–100,000 lux. They're a rough guide; a light meter app can measure a spot.

6. Can it tell me what plant I have?

No. The app doesn't identify plants or look anything up; you name each plant and set how often it needs water.

7. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
