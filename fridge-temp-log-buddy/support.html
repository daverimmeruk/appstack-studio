Fridge Temp Log Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Can the phone measure my fridge's temperature?

No. Read the unit's own thermometer (or a probe thermometer) and type the temperature in.

2. Which temperatures are OK?

Fridges: 5°C or colder is the usual advice, and 8°C is the legal maximum in England, Wales and Northern Ireland. Freezers: -18°C or colder. Hot holding: 63°C or hotter.

3. A reading was out of range. What now?

The app says what to check. Fix the cause, decide what to do with the food, check again, and type what you did: it is kept with the reading and printed on the record sheet. If you chose Save, action later, tap the reading in 📋 Log › ✏️ Add what you did.

4. How do I show my records to an inspector?

📄 Reports › 📄 This week's record sheet (or a month with Pro). Print it, or show the PDF on the phone.

5. The reminder came late or didn't come.

Android may deliver it up to about 10 minutes late to save battery. It is skipped if every unit has already been checked. If Android blocks the app's notifications, 📄 Reports shows 🔕 Notifications are blocked: tap it to allow them.

6. I entered a wrong reading.

Tap it in 📋 Log › 🗑 Delete (only a mistake), then enter the right one.

7. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
