Speed & GPS Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. It says "Waiting for GPS".

GPS needs a view of the sky. Go outside or near a window and wait up to a minute. Check that Location is on in the phone's quick settings.

2. Why is the GPS speed different from my car's?

Car speedometers usually read a little high, and GPS can lag by a second or two or jump when the signal is poor. Your car's speedometer and the road signs come first.

3. Does it track me in the background?

No. The app uses location only while it's open on screen. Trips pause if you switch to another app and carry on when you come back.

4. The distance looks low for my trip.

The app only adds distance from good GPS fixes (±25 m or better), so it doesn't count GPS drift while you stand still. In tunnels or with a poor signal some distance can be missed.

5. How does the HUD work?

At night, lay the phone flat on the dashboard (in a non-slip holder) with its top edge towards the windscreen, with the brightness up. The mirrored speed reflects in the glass the right way round. HUD is part of Pro.

6. What's a GPX file?

A standard trip file that maps and fitness apps can open (Pro). It contains the trip's positions, so only share it with people you trust.

7. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
