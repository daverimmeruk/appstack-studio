Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I set up Home Anti-Theft Guardian?

1. Open the Guardian tab and tap 'Scan & Select Home Wi-Fi'.
2. Tap 'Find & Connect Car Bluetooth' to pair your car headunit.
3. Tap 'Save Settings' and toggle 'Enable Home Guardian' to ON.

Q: Do I need to enter my Wi-Fi router password?

No! The app never asks for or stores your Wi-Fi password. It only checks if your phone's network state matches your Home Wi-Fi network name (SSID).

Q: How does the Wi-Fi Parking Radar work underground?

In multi-story or underground car parks where GPS is unavailable, the Radar measures Wi-Fi RSSI signal fingerprints and detects parking floor levels (-1, Basement) to guide you back to your car.
