Frequently Asked Questions

How do I mark my car?

On the free '🗺️ Outside Map & AR' tab, tap '📍 Mark Parked Car Here' and hold your phone steady during the 5-second countdown. When you come back, follow the compass arrow and the live distance to your car.

Why does the arrow stay still when I'm near my car?

While you are within 10 metres of your car, the arrow stays still and shows '🚶 Walk away to begin live tracking'. Live tracking starts once you walk more than 10 metres away.

The app says '⚠️ Compass disturbed by metal'. What should I do?

Cars, electricity pylons and other metal can throw a phone's compass off. Keep walking at a normal pace: the app corrects the arrow within a few seconds using your GPS direction of travel. If it still seems wrong, move the phone in a figure-8 away from metal.

What does the Underground Parking Radar do?

GPS often fails in multi-storey and underground car parks. When you mark your bay, the Radar (Pro) saves the Wi-Fi networks your phone can see, the air pressure (if your phone has a barometer) and your level and bay note. On your return, 'Start Radar Scan' tells you whether you are higher or lower than your car and how closely the Wi-Fi matches your bay. If the car park has no Wi-Fi or your phone has no barometer, the app says so rather than guessing.

How do I buy, restore or cancel Pro?

Pro is sold through Google Play as a one-time lifetime purchase or a monthly subscription. Prices and any free trial are shown by Google Play at checkout. Purchases are restored automatically on reinstall, or tap Restore on the Pro screen. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Why does the app need location permission?

To record where your car is and guide you back to it. Android also requires location permission before an app can scan nearby Wi-Fi networks, which the Underground Radar uses. Location is only used while the app is open.

Is my location uploaded anywhere?

No. Your car's position and parking data stay on your phone. See our Privacy Policy.
