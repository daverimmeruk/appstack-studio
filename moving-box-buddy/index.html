👋 What Moving Box Buddy does

Moving Box Buddy keeps a list of your moving boxes: a number for each box, the room it goes to at the new home, what's inside and a photo. It prints labels with a big number and the room's colour, and finds which box anything is in.

📦 Adding a box

On 📦 Boxes, tap ＋ Add a box. Choose the room it goes to, then type what's inside, one thing per line (for example "Kettle", "Mugs", "Tea towels"). Tick Fragile or Open first if needed.

The app gives each box the next number. Write the number on the box, or print the labels.

Then take a photo of what's inside before you close it, or skip it. Tap a box later to add things, add a photo, mark it unpacked or delete it.

🎨 Rooms and colours

Each room has its own colour, shown on the box list and printed on the labels. Put a matching sticker or a coloured paper on each room's door at the new home, so helpers know where boxes go.

Tap 🎨 Rooms on 📦 Boxes to add a room, rename one or change its colour.

🔍 Find

On 🔍 Find, type what you're looking for (for example "charger"). The app shows every box with that word in its contents, room or note, and the matching lines. You can also type a box number.

🏷️ Labels

On 📄 Print, tap 🏷️ Box labels. The PDF has two labels on each A4 page: cut along the dashed line and tape one on the side of each box.

Each label shows the box number, the room in its colour, FRAGILE or OPEN FIRST, the contents and "Box 3 of 24". Add your new address or a name and the moving date in 📄 Print to print them at the bottom.

No printer? Write the box number and room on the box: the app still knows what's inside.

✅ Unpacking

As you unpack, tap a box and choose ✅ Unpacked. The 📦 Boxes tab shows how many are still to unpack, and unpacked boxes move to the end of the list.

📄 Contents list and spreadsheet

  • 🏷️ Box labels (free).
  • 📄 Contents list PDF (Pro): every box with its room and contents, and the photos. Handy for insurance or a removal company.
  • 📊 Spreadsheet (Pro): every box as a CSV for Excel or Google Sheets.

⭐ Free and Pro

Free: up to 30 boxes, contents and photos, search, room colours and the box labels. No ads.

Pro (one payment, no subscription): unlimited boxes, the contents list PDF and the spreadsheet.

Tap GO PRO at the top. Restore purchase gets Pro back on a new phone with the same Google account.

🔒 Privacy

No account, no sign-in, and no ads. Your boxes and photos are kept only in the app's own storage on this phone. Moving Box Buddy has no servers. A PDF or spreadsheet only leaves the phone when you share it yourself.

Google Play handles the Pro purchase.
