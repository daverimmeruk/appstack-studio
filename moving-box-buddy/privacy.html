Moving Box Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.movingboxbuddy | Effective Date: 19 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Moving Box Buddy keeps a list of your moving boxes. Your boxes and photos stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play's billing library sends Google its own diagnostic logs, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Boxes: the box number, the room it goes to, what's inside, Fragile / Open first / Unpacked, a note, and the photos you take or choose (stored as smaller copies, at most 1600 pixels across).
  • Rooms: the room names and label colours.
  • Settings: the address or name and the moving date you choose to print on the labels, whether the welcome guide was shown and whether Pro is unlocked.

3. Photos

Photos come from your phone's own camera app or from Android's photo picker, which only gives the app the picture you choose. The app has no camera or storage permission and never reads your other photos.

4. Permissions Used

  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No notifications, no location, contacts, microphone, SMS or call log, no alarms, no overlay, no accessibility service and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a box added or labels made), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. PDFs, Spreadsheet and Manual

Your data only leaves your phone when you share the labels, a PDF, the spreadsheet (Pro) or the manual. It is then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen, for example a printer app or a removal company by email.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a box with its photos by tapping it, then 🗑 Delete. Delete a single photo from 🖼️ See the photos.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Moving Box Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Moving Box Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes and Contact
