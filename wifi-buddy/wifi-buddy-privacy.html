Wi-Fi Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.wifibuddy | Effective Date: 13 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Wi-Fi Buddy shows the Wi-Fi networks your phone can hear, the channels they use and the signal of the network you're connected to. Everything the app saves is stored on your phone only. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account, and it has no ads and no analytics.

2. Your Location

  • Android only gives Wi-Fi scan results to apps with precise location permission, and only while Location is switched on. Wi-Fi Buddy asks for location for that reason.
  • The map at the bottom of the Networks tab shows where you are, on your phone only. Wi-Fi Buddy never saves your location, never puts it in exports and never sends it anywhere.
  • You can say no: the Signal tab still shows your connection's signal, but Android then hides network names and scan results.

3. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Saved scans (Pro): the networks in a scan you chose to save: network name, router address (BSSID), signal, band, channel, width, security and Wi-Fi standard, and the time.
  • Room readings (Pro): the room name you typed, the connected network's name and band, and the measured signal and link speed.
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, whether the demo is on, and whether Pro is unlocked.

Live scans and your connection details (IP address, gateway, DNS) are shown on screen and are not saved.

Map pictures downloaded from OpenStreetMap are kept in a cache inside the app on your phone, so the map loads faster next time. They are map pictures only, not your location.

4. Permissions and Access Used

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): needed by Android for Wi-Fi scan results, and used to show your position on the map on your phone. Never saved or sent.
  • Wi-Fi state (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE): to read the networks Android reports and to ask Android for a new scan. Wi-Fi Buddy never connects to, disconnects from or changes any network.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): to see whether you're connected to Wi-Fi and to read the phone's IP address, gateway and DNS.
  • Internet: to download the map pictures from OpenStreetMap, and used by Google Play's billing library for the Pro purchase.
  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase.

The app does not use your contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, photos or the phone permission.

5. Third-Party Services

  • OpenStreetMap: the map pictures (tiles) are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's servers. Like any map, their server sees which map area is downloaded and your internet (IP) address. Wi-Fi Buddy does not send your Wi-Fi details or saved data. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy.

6. CSV and PDF Exports

Scans and room readings only leave your phone when you tap CSV or PDF (Pro, or with the demo's sample data) and share the file. They are then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen. Exports never include your location.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a saved scan from its banner (📂 Saved). Delete a room with its bin, or all rooms with Clear in the Rooms tab.
  • Wi-Fi Buddy keeps up to 50 saved scans; when full, the oldest is removed first.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Wi-Fi Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Wi-Fi Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone, so it stays on this phone only.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it stores or shares, we will update this page and its effective date.
