❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


Q1: Why does a Wi-Fi app need my location?

Android only gives the list of nearby Wi-Fi networks to apps with precise location permission, and only while Location is switched on. That's Android's rule for every Wi-Fi app. Wi-Fi Buddy shows your position on the map on your phone only, and never saves or sends your location. See our Privacy Policy.

Q2: Why am I not seeing any networks?

Follow the card at the top of the app: allow location (choose Precise), switch Location on, and switch Wi-Fi on. Then tap 🔄 Scan. Weak networks don't appear in every scan, so scan again a little later.

Q3: Why does it say "Next scan in …s"?

Android allows an app 4 Wi-Fi scans every 2 minutes. Wi-Fi Buddy scans by itself every 40 seconds while Networks or Channels is open, and shows when the results are from, so you never see old results passed off as new.

Q4: What do Excellent, Strong, Good and Weak mean?

Wi-Fi Buddy uses the same Coverage maths as Cell Tower Finder and CellCoverage: Coverage score = (dBm + 100), counted from 0 to 60 as 0 to 100%. 🌟 Excellent is 90% or more (-46 dBm or better) and flashes green on the Signal tab; 🟢 Strong is 75 to 89%; 🟡 Good is 50 to 74%; 🔴 Weak is below 50%.

Q5: Can Wi-Fi Buddy change my router's channel or make my Wi-Fi faster?

No. Wi-Fi Buddy only measures. It shows which channels are crowded where you are and where your signal is weak, so you can change the channel in your router's settings or app, or move the router.

Q6: Is the link speed my internet speed?

No. Link speed is the radio link between your phone and the router. Your internet speed also depends on your broadband connection.

Q7: What does Pro include?

Free includes the network list, the channel graph, your connection, the live signal meter, the map and the demo. Pro adds room comparison, saved scans, CSV export and the PDF report.

Q8: How do I buy or restore Pro?

Pro is one payment for life through Google Play, with no subscription. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. On a new phone with the same Google account, tap GO PRO and then Restore purchase.

Q9: Why is the map an empty grey grid?

The map pictures come from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org), so they need the internet. If they can't download, Wi-Fi Buddy shows a message over the map. Check your internet connection. An ad-blocker, VPN or private DNS can block the map: allow tile.openstreetmap.org in it, or switch it off for Wi-Fi Buddy. Everything else in Wi-Fi Buddy works without the map.
