QR Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.qrbuddy | Effective Date: 26 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

QR Buddy scans and makes QR codes and barcodes. Your scan history and saved codes stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play services and Google Play's billing library send Google their own diagnostic information, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Scan history: what each scanned code held, its barcode format and the time it was scanned. Free keeps the newest 20 scans; Pro keeps them all.
  • Saved codes: the QR codes you make and choose to save: their label and what they hold (for example a Wi-Fi network name and password you typed).
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many useful results you have had (used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating).

3. Scanning and Making Codes

The camera screen is Google's code scanner (Google ML Kit), part of Google Play services on your phone. It runs on the phone: the app only receives what the scanned code says, never the camera picture, and has no camera permission. When you scan a code in a photo, Android's photo picker gives the app only the picture you choose; the picture is read on the phone and not kept.

Links are checked on the phone by looking at the address only; nothing is looked up online, and a link only opens in your browser when you tap Open. QR codes are made on the phone with the open-source ZXing library. The first time, Google Play services may download the scanner to your phone.

4. Permissions Used

  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No camera, storage, contacts, location, phone, SMS, Wi-Fi or notification permission. Adding a contact, joining a Wi-Fi network, calling or emailing opens Android's own screen or your own app, where you decide.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google ML Kit (in Google Play services): the code scanner and the barcode reader for photos. Google states that ML Kit may send Google information about how well these features work (for example which device and how long a task took), but not the pictures or what the codes say. See Google's ML Kit terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a code scanned or shared), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. Sharing

Something only leaves your phone when you choose: sharing a code as a picture, a printable card (Pro), the history spreadsheet (Pro), a result's text or the manual; or opening a link, calling, emailing, messaging or joining a network from a result.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a scan from its result screen, or 🗑 Clear history to delete all scans. Delete a saved code from its screen.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › QR Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

QR Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
