❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


Q1: How do I find the best spot for my 5G/4G router?

Open the 'Step 1: Cell' screen and walk around your home with the app open, watching the signal level: the higher (closer to 0 dBm), the better. Windows facing your mast usually read best. Then use 📶 Coverage and 📐 Blueprint to compare rooms.

Q2: Why doesn't the map show cell towers?

Android does not report where cell towers are, so towers are not drawn. The map shows your own signal readings as colour-coded rings wherever you have walked with the app open. Tap '5G Heatmap' to see where you had 5G.

Q3: What does purple mean on the 5G Heatmap?

Purple means you were on 5G, but your phone does not report the 5G signal level (some phones, e.g. Samsung, don't). Grey means no 5G (4G only).

Q4: How do I find a weak room and where to put a mesh node?

Tap '📶 Coverage', walk to the room furthest from your router and tap '📶 Test Room Reception'. A red result means a dead-zone where an extender or mesh node is recommended. '📶 Extender Node Guide' shows where to place it: about halfway between your router and the dead-zone.

Q5: What does Pro include?

Step 1, the Map and 📶 Coverage are free. Pro adds 📐 Blueprint (record readings room by room and compare rooms on a leaderboard) and PDF report exports.

Q6: How do I buy, restore or cancel Pro?

Pro is bought through Google Play as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices and any free trial are shown by Google Play at checkout. Purchases are restored automatically on reinstall. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Q7: Why does the app need location permission?

Android requires location permission before any app can read cell details such as band and signal level. The app also uses it to place your readings on the map. Your readings stay on your phone; see our Privacy Policy.

Q8: Why does another phone show a different signal?

The radio waves are the same, but phones differ in their modems, antennas and how their software smooths readings. Two phones side by side can read differently from the same tower. Please use the app's readings as a guide.
