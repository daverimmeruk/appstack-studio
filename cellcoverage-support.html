❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


Q1: How do I align my 5G router using the 360° Compass Radar?

Hold your phone horizontally and rotate 360°. The arrow needle dynamically points directly toward your connected 5G cell tower. When the needle locks on target (< 10° delta), it turns neon green with 🎯 Line-of-Sight: 100% Locked. Position your 5G/LTE gateway at the window facing this exact azimuth direction.

Q2: How do I switch radio bands or lock onto alternative cell towers?

On the Nearby Towers tab, tap ⚙️ Switch Band. This opens Android's hidden Radio Info testing screen (*#*#4636#*#*). Under 'Set Preferred Network Type', select 'LTE Only' or 'NR/LTE' to force your modem onto alternative backup towers.

Q3: How do I export and share my signal field report?

On the Compare tab, tap Share PDF Report. You can instantly send your 3GPP telemetry field report via Email, WhatsApp, Drive, or Bluetooth.

Q4: How does the 3-Day Free Trial work?

You can unlock all WaveScan Pro features (360° Compass Radar, Nearby Towers, Operator Comparison, and PDF Export) with a 3-Day Free Trial handled securely through Google Play Console.
