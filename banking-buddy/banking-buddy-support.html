❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)


Q1: Why am I not seeing any alerts?

Banking Buddy can only show alerts your banking apps already send. Open each banking app and switch on its notifications (payment, transaction or spending alerts), and check Android lets that app show notifications. In Banking Buddy, check the top card is green ("Banking Buddy is on"), the bank's switch is on in the Banks tab, and your Filters aren't switching that kind of alert off.

Q2: Does Banking Buddy need my bank login?

No. Banking Buddy never asks for bank logins or passwords and does not connect to your bank. It reads the notifications your banking apps show on your phone.

Q3: Where are my alerts stored?

On your phone only. They are not backed up and the app never sends them anywhere. They only leave your phone when you tap Share or export a CSV. See our Privacy Policy.

Q4: Why is an alert marked Money out when it was money in?

Banking Buddy works out money in / money out from the words in the bank's alert, so an unusually worded alert can be marked wrongly. The bank's own message is always shown in full, so you can check it.

Q5: How do regular payments work?

In Logs, tap 🔁 Regular on an alert, give it a name such as Salary or Car loan, and check the words to look for (for example the payer's name). New alerts from the same banking app with those words get the name automatically, and the Regular tab shows the last amount and whether it has been seen this month.

Q6: What does Pro include?

Free includes money in / money out alerts from 2 banking apps, the last 30 days of history, notes, sharing and 2 regular payments. Pro adds unlimited banking apps and regular payments, full history, CSV export and monthly totals.

Q7: How do I buy or restore Pro?

Pro is one payment for life through Google Play, with no subscription. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. On a new phone with the same Google account, tap GO PRO and then Restore purchase.

Q8: Do the totals match my bank statement?

Not always. Totals are added up from the amounts written in the alerts your phone received. Alerts without an amount, or alerts your bank didn't send, aren't counted. Always check your banking app or statement for your real balance.
