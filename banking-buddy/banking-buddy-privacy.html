Banking Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.bankingbuddy.app | Effective Date: 12 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Banking Buddy collects the money in / money out notifications from the banking apps you choose into one list on your phone. Everything the app saves is stored on your phone only. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and no bank logins or passwords, and it has no ads and no analytics.

Banking Buddy is not a bank and is not connected to or affiliated with any bank.

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Bank alerts: for each notification from a banking app you switched on: the bank app's name, the notification's title and text (which can include amounts and payee names), the time, and whether it looks like money in, money out or a notice.
  • Your additions: notes you type, alerts you mark ⭐ Saved, and the names and words of regular payments you set up (for example "Salary").
  • Settings: the banking apps you chose, your filter settings, and whether Pro is unlocked.

Notifications from any app you have not added and switched on are ignored straight away and never saved.

3. Permissions and Access Used

  • Notification access (BIND_NOTIFICATION_LISTENER_SERVICE): lets Banking Buddy see the alerts your banking apps show. Banking Buddy explains this before you turn it on, and you switch it on yourself in Android's Notification access settings. You can switch it off there at any time.
  • List of apps on your phone: used only to show your apps (names and icons) when you tap + Bank, so you can choose your banking apps. Only the apps you choose are saved.
  • Google Play Billing, internet and network state: used by Google Play's billing library to handle the Pro purchase. Banking Buddy never sends your alerts, notes or settings over the internet.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or the phone permission.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Sharing and CSV Export

Alerts only leave your phone when you tap Share or export a CSV file (Pro). They are then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

6. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete one alert with 🗑️ in Logs. Clear deletes all alerts except ⭐ Saved ones. Regular payments can be deleted in the Regular tab.
  • Banking Buddy keeps up to 1,000 alerts; when full, the oldest unsaved ones are removed first.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Banking Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • Banking Buddy's data is not included in Android backups and is not copied to a new phone, so it stays on this phone only.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

7. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it stores or shares, we will update this page and its effective date.
