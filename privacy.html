1. Overview

This page covers what all AppStack Studio apps have in common. Each app also has its own privacy policy that lists exactly what it reads, stores and sends (see section 6). If the two ever differ, the app's own policy is the one that applies.

2. What all our apps have in common

  • No account: none of our apps asks you to sign up or log in.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data.
  • Stored on your phone: the readings, settings and reports an app saves are kept in its private storage on your phone. We have no servers that receive them.
  • Permissions are used for the app's own features only, for example location for a map, the camera for a camera view, or the microphone for a sound measurement. Each app's policy explains its permissions.
  • Exports: a PDF, CSV or other file is only made when you tap the button, and only shared with the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

3. When an app uses the internet

  • Map pictures: apps with a map download map pictures (tiles) from OpenStreetMap. Like any website, OpenStreetMap's servers receive your IP address and which map area is loaded.
  • Cell Tower Finder's speed test: only when you run it, test data is downloaded from and uploaded to Cloudflare's public speed-test service (speed.cloudflare.com).
  • Phone Check Buddy's check codes: only when you make or answer a check code, the phone's report is locked (AES-256) with a key made from the code and passed through Google Cloud Firestore (Google Firebase, London). We can't read it, it is deleted as soon as the code is used, and it can't be opened after 7 days.
  • Game Ready Buddy's connection test: only when you run it, small STUN requests (the "what is my address" packets games and video calls use) go to the server you pick, Cloudflare (stun.cloudflare.com) or Google (stun.l.google.com). That server sees your internet (IP) address; the packets contain nothing about you or your phone.
  • Scam Check Buddy's online link check: only when you tap it, the domain name of each web address (never the rest of the address or your message) is sent to the official domain register for that ending, and a short link is sent to the short-link service it belongs to. The website itself is never contacted.
  • Purchases: Pro and credits are bought through Google Play.

4. Purchases

Payments are handled by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment details. Prices are shown by Google Play at checkout. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Deleting your data

Uninstalling an app, or Android Settings › Apps › [the app] › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything that app has stored on your phone. As we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete; if you have a question about your data, email us.

7. Our YouTube channel and video uploader (YouTube API Services)

We publish short demonstration videos of our own apps on our YouTube channel, Appstack Studio (@AppstackStudio). To upload them we use our own small tool, AppStack Studio Uploader, which uses YouTube API Services. Our Android apps themselves do not use YouTube API Services.

  • Who uses it: only us, the channel owner, signed in to our own Google account. It is not offered to anyone else, and no viewer or other user signs in to it.
  • What it does: it checks it is signed in to our own channel and uploads our finished videos with their title, description and tags. It uses two permissions: upload videos (youtube.upload) and view our own channel's name and ID (youtube.readonly).
  • What it keeps: only the ID and web address of each video it uploaded, on our own computer. It does not collect, store or share any information about viewers, comments, analytics or other YouTube users.
  • YouTube's own terms apply when you watch our videos on YouTube: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
  • Removing access and deleting data: the uploader's access to a Google account can be removed at any time on the Google security settings page (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). If you ask us by email at appstack1966@gmail.com, we delete any data the uploader has stored within 7 days. Its stored sign-in is also deleted if access is removed.

If what our apps read, store or send changes, we update this page, the app's own policy and the effective date.
