🗑️ Account & Data Deletion Policy

AppStack Studio apps don't use accounts or passwords, and we have no servers that store your data. What an app saves is kept on your phone. The only data that passes through an online service is a Phone Check Buddy report you choose to send with a check code: it is locked with the code so we can't read it, deleted as soon as the code is used, and can't be opened after 7 days.

To erase everything an app has stored on your phone:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps › [the app].
  3. Tap Storage › Clear storage (some phones say Clear data), or uninstall the app.
