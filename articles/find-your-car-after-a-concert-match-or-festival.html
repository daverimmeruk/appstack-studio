Find Your Car After a Concert, Match or Festival

Big events mean huge, unfamiliar car parks and fields full of identical rows, often in the dark. Here is how to mark your car in one tap on the way in and walk straight back to it afterwards.

🏠 Studio Home Car Finder ▶ Google Play

Why event parking is so easy to get lost in

Stadium car parks, showground fields and festival parking have no shops or landmarks to remember, the rows all look the same, and you come back hours later, often in the dark and with thousands of people leaving at once. A photo of a row sign helps only if there is a sign. A saved location on your phone works anywhere outside.

Step 1: Mark the car before you walk away

Open Car Finder on the Outside Map & AR tab and tap 📍 Mark Parked Car Here. Hold the phone steady for the 5-second satellite countdown while the app locks your spot. Extra taps during the countdown are ignored, and if a car is already saved somewhere else, the app asks before replacing it, so an accidental tap can't lose your car.

Your car appears on the map. While you are still within 10 metres of it the arrow stays still and shows "🚶 Walk away to begin live tracking", so the screen stays calm until you leave.

Step 2: Walk back with the arrow and live distance

When you come back, open the app. Once you are more than 10 metres away, the compass arrow guides you with the live distance (for example 🚗 35 m). Hold the phone flat or tilted towards you, with the top pointing the way you walk.

  • Turn until the arrow turns green: "🟢 Pointing at your car" and "🎯 Car is straight ahead: walk forward".
  • Within 3 metres it shows "😊 Car's location found!".
  • Rows of parked cars can throw a phone's compass off. If you see the amber "⚠️ Compass disturbed by metal" message, keep walking at a normal pace: the arrow corrects itself within a few seconds using your GPS direction of travel.

Tips for big events

  • Mark the car as soon as you park, before you join the crowd walking in.
  • Outdoors, GPS is usually accurate to a few metres; next to tall stands or trees it can be a little less precise, so look around once the distance gets small.
  • When you have found the car, tap 🔄 Reset Car's Location so it is ready for next time. The app asks first, so you can't clear it by accident.

Parked in a multi-storey instead?

Underground and multi-storey car parks block GPS. Car Finder Pro's Underground Parking Radar saves the Wi-Fi networks and air pressure at your bay, plus your note of the level and bay number, and tells you on your return whether you are on the right level and how closely the Wi-Fi matches your bay.

Outside Map & AR is free. The Underground Parking Radar is part of Pro, one payment for life through Google Play, with no subscription.
