Is My Phone Really on 5G? What the 5G Icon Means (NSA, SA and 5G+)

The 5G icon is on but it feels like 4G? Here is what your phone actually means by 5G, the difference between 5G NSA, 5G SA and 5G+, and how to check your real 5G status and signal on your own phone.

The 5G icon doesn't always mean what you think

When your phone shows 5G in the status bar, it means the phone is using a 5G connection, or that 5G is part of the connection. It doesn't tell you how strong that 5G signal is, or whether the phone is leaning mostly on 4G behind the scenes. That is why the icon can say 5G while a download feels no faster than 4G.

Android itself knows more than the icon shows. It reports which kind of 5G the phone is on, and on many phones the 5G signal level too.

The kinds of 5G your phone can report

  • 5G NSA (Non-Standalone): 5G with a 4G anchor. The phone keeps a 4G connection for control and adds 5G for data. Many networks, including in the UK, use this kind of 5G.

  • 5G SA (Standalone): the whole connection is 5G, with no 4G anchor.

  • 5G+: the high-capacity kind of 5G that some networks and phones show with a plus sign.

  • 5G heard, phone using 4G: your phone can hear a 5G signal but isn't using it right now. The icon may say 4G even though 5G is nearby.

  • No 5G here: 4G only: no 5G where you are, or none your phone can use.

Check your real 5G status on your own phone

Open CellCoverage 5g and tap 🗼 Step 1: Cell. The screen shows your live connection as measured by the phone: carrier, network (4G or 5G), band, signal level (RSRP), RSRQ, SINR and the cell IDs.

Under it, the 5G line shows exactly what the phone reports, for example "5G connected (NSA: 5G with a 4G anchor)" or "5G signal heard, phone using 4G". It reads the same information that drives the 5G icon in your status bar, so nothing is estimated.

When your phone also reports the 5G signal level, the line adds it, for example "5G signal -88 dBm · Good 5G":

  • Excellent 5G: -80 dBm or better.

  • Good 5G: down to -90 dBm.

  • Fair 5G: down to -100 dBm.

  • Weak 5G: below -100 dBm.

Not every phone reports a 5G signal level. If yours doesn't, the app says so instead of guessing.

Why 5G can be weak indoors

A lot of fast 5G runs on band n78 (3,500 MHz). Higher frequencies carry more data but don't pass through walls as well as 4G does. So 5G that is strong in the street can drop to weak, or to 4G, a few rooms inside.

  • Check the signal at a window facing the mast: 5G often reads much better there.

  • Walls, floors, metal and energy-saving glass all cut the signal.

  • If you use a 4G or 5G home router, put it where the 5G line reads best, usually a window on the side of the house facing the mast.

Seeing where you had 5G

CellCoverage 5g's Map shows your own signal readings as coloured rings wherever you have walked with the app open. Switch the map to 5G Heatmap to see where your phone was actually on 5G, not just where the signal was strong.
