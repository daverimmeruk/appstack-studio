Forgot Where You Parked? How to Find Your Car in a Big Outdoor Car Park

At festivals, stadiums, airports and retail parks every row looks the same. Here is how to mark your car in seconds and follow an arrow back to it with Car Finder, free.

Why it's easy to lose your car outside

At a festival, a football ground, an airport or a big retail park, every row looks the same, and the signs you noticed on the way in are gone when you come back. A map app can show where you are, but not where you left the car, unless you saved it first.

1. Mark the spot before you walk off (free)

Open Car Finder, tap 📍 Mark Parked Car Here and hold your phone steady during the 5-second countdown while it locks your position. The ±m shown next to it is the accuracy your phone reported for that spot.

Two things stop you losing the spot by mistake: extra taps during the countdown are ignored, and if a car is already saved and you are well away from it, the app asks before replacing it.

2. Walk away

While you are within 10 metres of the car, the compass stays still and shows 🚶 Walk away to begin live tracking, so the screen is calm while you lock the car and get going.

3. Follow the arrow back

When you come back, open the app. Once you are more than 10 metres away, the AR compass shows the live distance to your car (for example 🚗 35m) and which way to turn. Hold the phone flat or tilted towards you, with the top pointing the way you walk.

  • Turn until the needle and crosshairs go green: 🎯 Car is straight ahead: walk forward.
  • Within 3 metres it shows 😊 Car's location found!
  • The map (OpenStreetMap) shows a car marker where you parked.

When the arrow seems wrong

A phone's compass can be pulled off by metal: other cars, electricity pylons and steel structures. Car Finder compares the magnetic field your phone measures with the field expected where you are, and shows an amber ⚠️ Compass disturbed by metal message when something is wrong. Keep walking at a normal pace: the arrow corrects itself within a few seconds using your GPS direction of travel.

If it still seems off, move the phone in a figure-8, away from metal, to recalibrate the compass.

Tips

  • Mark the car as soon as you park, before you walk off.
  • GPS is less accurate between tall buildings and under trees, and some phones have better GPS than others, so look around when you are within a few metres.
  • When you are back at the car, tap 🔄 Reset Car's Location. The app asks first, so an accidental tap can't lose your car.

Parked indoors?
