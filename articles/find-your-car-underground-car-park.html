How to Find Your Car in an Underground Car Park When GPS Stops Working

GPS often fails in underground and multi-storey car parks. Here is how Car Finder uses Wi-Fi and air pressure to help you get back to your bay.

🏠 Studio Home Car Finder ▶ Google Play

Why your phone gets lost in car parks

Concrete floors and ceilings block satellite signals, so the blue dot in a normal map app drifts or disappears underground. In a multi-storey car park it also can't tell which level you are on.

Outside: mark it and follow the arrow (free)

When you park, tap 📍 Mark Parked Car Here and hold your phone steady during the 5-second countdown. Once you walk more than 10 metres away, the AR compass wakes up and shows the live distance and which way to turn. When the needle and crosshairs turn green, your car is straight ahead.

Cars, electricity pylons and other metal can throw a phone's compass off. Car Finder warns you when that happens, and the arrow corrects itself within a few seconds as you keep walking, using your GPS direction of travel.

Underground: Wi-Fi and air pressure (Pro)

When you park, open the 📡 Underg. Radar tab (Underground Parking Radar) and tap 📍 Mark Parked Car Here. You can type your level and bay too, for example "Level B2, bay 47". Car Finder saves:

  • the Wi-Fi networks your phone can see at the bay
  • the air pressure, if your phone has a barometer
  • your note, plus a GPS position if one is available

When you come back, tap Start Radar Scan. The app compares fresh readings with the saved ones and tells you whether you are higher or lower than your car (in metres and approximate levels) and how closely the Wi-Fi matches your bay.

How the level check works

Air pressure drops by about 0.12 hPa for every metre you go up. Comparing the pressure now with the pressure at your bay gives the height difference, and car park levels are roughly 3 m each. Weather slowly changes air pressure too, so readings taken many hours apart are approximate.

If the car park has no Wi-Fi or your phone has no barometer, the app says so rather than guessing.

Tips

  • Mark the bay as soon as you park, before you walk off.
  • Type the level and bay number: the app shows it back to you.
  • Underground, trust the level and Wi-Fi match more than the arrow, because the arrow uses GPS.
