How to Find the Best Spot for Your 4G or 5G Router at Home

Slow 4G/5G home internet often comes down to where the router sits. Here is how to find the strongest spot in your home from your own phone's live signal.

Why router position matters

A 4G or 5G home router gets its internet from a mobile mast, just like your phone. Walls, floors, metal and foil-backed insulation all weaken that signal, so the same router can work well by one window and badly in the next room. Moving it to the right spot can make a real difference, and it costs nothing.

Step 1: Walk round your home with your phone

Open Cell Tower Finder and tap 🗼 Cell Tower. Walk slowly round the house and garden, front and back. Every reading your phone takes becomes a coloured circle on the map: 🟢 strong, 🟡 fair, 🔴 weak. That builds a live heatmap of your own signal.

Step 2: Find the ⭐ strong spot

The app marks a ⭐ strong spot where it has measured the best signal so far, and the big compass points to it. The ⭐ only moves when a new spot is clearly stronger (3 dB or more), so it doesn't jump about with every reading. The ⭐ spot is a good place for your phone or your 4G/5G router.

Once you have walked round the outside of your home, the small arrow shows which way your mobile signal gets stronger. That is usually the side facing the mast, but walls and reflections can mislead it, so treat it as a guide.

GPS indoors is only accurate to about 5–15 m, so the heatmap tells you which side or end of the house is best rather than which shelf.

Step 3: Check the signal numbers

Signal strength is measured in dBm: the closer to zero, the better. For 4G/5G (RSRP):

  • -80 dBm or better: excellent
  • -80 to -90 dBm: good
  • -90 to -100 dBm: fair, so try the router closer to a window
  • Below -100 dBm: poor; an external antenna, a booster or Wi-Fi Calling may help

Step 4: Check your Wi-Fi reaches the rooms you use

Once the router is in its best spot, the next question is how far its Wi-Fi reaches. Tap 📷 Camera AR and move slowly from spot to spot. The crosshairs flash green at a Coverage score of 75% or more ("🎯 OPTIMAL ROUTER SPOT!"), turn amber from 50% and red below that. Everything above is free.

With Pro you can also compare rooms side by side with 📐 Blueprint, and test the rooms furthest from the router with 📶 Cellular Coverage, which shows where a Wi-Fi extender or mesh node would help.

Your readings stay on your phone

Cell Tower Finder doesn't rely on crowd-sourced tower databases, which can be approximate or out of date. It works from what your own phone measures right now, and your readings are stored only on your phone.
