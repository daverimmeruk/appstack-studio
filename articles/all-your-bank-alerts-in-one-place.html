Accounts at Several Banks? How to See Every Money-In and Money-Out Alert in One Place

A current account at one bank, savings at another, a credit card somewhere else: here is how to switch on each bank's payment alerts and keep every one of them in one list on your phone, without giving any app your bank login.

Three banks, three apps, three sets of alerts

Many people have a current account at one bank, savings at another and a credit card or an online-only account somewhere else. Each banking app sends its own alerts when money comes in or goes out, and each one keeps them in its own place. To see what happened this week, you open three apps and scroll through three lists. An alert you swiped away is simply gone.

Step 1: switch on the alerts inside each banking app

Every list starts with the alerts your banks send, so check they are on. In most banking apps they are under Settings, Notifications or Alerts. Look for options such as:

  • Payments in (salary, transfers, refunds)

  • Payments out or card payments

  • Direct debits and standing orders

Switch on the ones you want. Some banks only send an alert above an amount you choose: set it low if you want to see small card payments too.

Step 2: keep them all in one list

Banking Buddy collects those alerts into one list on your phone. Tap ＋ Bank, choose your banking apps, then tap Turn on and allow notification access. From then on, every money-in and money-out alert from those apps is saved in Logs, marked 💚 Money in, ❤️ Money out or 🔔 Notice.

It does not connect to your bank and never asks for a bank login or password. It can only see the alerts your banking apps already show on your phone, and only from the apps you choose: notifications from every other app are ignored and never saved. The alerts stay on your phone and only leave it if you share them.

What you can do with one list

  • Search by shop, payee, amount or note: find that payment from three weeks ago in seconds.

  • Add a note, or tap a quick one: Salary, Rent, Bills, Shopping, Business, Transfer.

  • ⭐ Save the important ones so they are never cleared.

  • 🔁 Regular payments: name a payment that comes round, like Salary or a loan, and see whether it has arrived this month.

  • Any currency: £, €, $, ¥, ₹, zł, kr and the rest are found in the alerts, with totals kept apart for each currency.

With Pro (one payment, no subscription) you can add as many banking apps as you like, keep the full history, see money in and out by week, month or year, and export a spreadsheet for Excel, Google Sheets or your accountant.

Good to know

  • Alerts that arrived before you turned it on can't be added: the list starts from today.

  • Totals are added up from the amounts written in the alerts, so they can differ from your bank statement if an alert has no amount.

  • Banking Buddy is not a bank. Your bank's own app and statement are always the official record.

In short

Switch on payment alerts in each banking app, then let Banking Buddy keep them all in one searchable list on your phone, with notes and totals, without ever giving it your bank login.
