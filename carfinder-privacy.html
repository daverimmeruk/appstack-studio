Privacy Policy for Car Finder & Anti-Theft Guardian

Effective Date: October 2026

At AppStack Studio, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how Car Finder & Anti-Theft Guardian handles your information.

1. Information We Collect & Store Locally

Car Finder is designed with privacy-first principles. All sensitive credentials—including your Home Wi-Fi SSID, Car Bluetooth MAC address, and Parked GPS Coordinates—are stored 100% locally on your device in secure private storage. We do not upload, collect, or transmit your personal location or network data to any remote server.

2. Permissions Used

  • Location (Fine/Coarse): Used strictly on-device to record your parked car's GPS coordinates, measure walking distance, and power the AR compass and Wi-Fi parking radar.
  • Wi-Fi & Network State: Used locally to detect whether your phone is connected to your Home Wi-Fi network for the Anti-Theft Guardian feature.
  • Bluetooth: Used locally to detect connection and disconnection from your paired car Bluetooth headunit.
  • Notifications & Full-Screen Intent: Used locally on-device to sound the theft alarm and wake up your screen if unauthorized movement is detected.

3. Third-Party Services & Billing

If you choose to upgrade to the PRO Suite or start a 3-day free trial, in-app purchases are securely processed via Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your financial or credit card information.
