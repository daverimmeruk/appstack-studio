Privacy Policy for Car Finder

App: Car Finder (com.appstackstudio.carfinder)
Developer: AppStack Studio
Effective Date: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what Car Finder stores, what leaves your phone, and how to delete it. In short: everything the app records stays on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data.

1. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Parked car position: when you tap '📍 Mark Parked Car Here', the GPS latitude and longitude of your car.
  • Underground Parking Radar (Pro): when you mark your bay, the Wi-Fi networks your phone can see (their access-point identifiers, called BSSIDs, and their signal levels), the air pressure from your phone's barometer (if it has one), the level and bay note you type, and a GPS position if one is available.
  • App settings: for example whether you have seen the tips and whether Pro is unlocked.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your phone. It is not uploaded to us or anyone else. The app needs no account, and it does not ask for your name, email address or contacts. It has no ads and no analytics.

2. Permissions Used

  • Location (precise and approximate): to mark your car, to show the distance and direction while you walk back, and to scan nearby Wi-Fi networks for the Underground Radar (Android requires location permission for Wi-Fi scans). Location is used only while the app is open, never in the background.
  • Wi-Fi state (view and change): to scan the Wi-Fi networks around your bay when you park and when you return. The app does not connect to any network and never reads Wi-Fi passwords.
  • Internet and network state: to download map images and to check purchases with Google Play.
  • Sensors: the compass, motion sensors and barometer need no permission. They are read on your phone only.

3. Third-Party Services

  • OpenStreetMap: map images (tiles) are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address, the app's name and which map area is being loaded. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. The price is shown by Google Play at checkout.
  • Android location services: your position comes from your phone's location service (Google Play services), which follows your phone's location settings and Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Your saved car and bay stay on your phone until you replace them. Tap '🔄 Reset Car's Location' to clear both the Map and Underground Radar data.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Car Finder › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored.
  • If Android backup to your Google account is turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own backup. This is controlled by your phone's settings.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

5. Changes to This Policy

If the app changes what it stores or shares, we will update this page and its effective date.
