🌟 PART 1: FREE EDITION WORKFLOW (STEPS 1 & 2)

• Step 1 (🗼 Cell Tower Map - Outdoor Alignment)

  1. Open the app and tap the '🗼 Cell Tower' tab.
  2. Hold your phone horizontally at chest height and slowly rotate 360° in a circle.
  3. Watch the Directional Pointer Arrow on screen:
    • If not facing the tower, it displays: '➔ Turn Right X°' or '⬅ Turn Left X°' in red text.
    • When facing the cell tower (within ±10°), it locks to BRIGHT NEON GREEN: '🎯 Line-of-Sight: 100% Locked'.
  4. Read the Signal Power (dBm) and Distance (meters). Identify the window in your building facing that exact azimuth angle.

• Step 2 (📷 Camera AR Alignment - Outdoor & Indoor Modes)

  1. Tap '📷 AR Camera' and select '📡 Cell Tower AR' at the top.
  2. Point your camera out exterior windows. The 3D Direction Arrow overlays live in camera view, pointing towards the exact GPS location of the cell tower.
  3. Rotate your camera until the reticle turns green with '🎯 100% LINE-OF-SIGHT ALIGNED WITH CELL TOWER'.
  4. Tap '🏠 Switch AR Mode' or '🏠 Indoor Router AR' at the top to toggle to Indoor Router AR Placement Mode.
  5. Point your camera at your designated router spot near the window. A floating 3D anchor box overlays in camera view, displaying 1.2m elevation callouts, wall barrier advice, and optimal router positioning.

💎 PART 2: WAVESCAN PRO INDOOR SUITE (STEPS 3 & 4)

• Step 3 (📐 Room Selection & Blueprint Modeler)

  1. Position your 5G/LTE Gateway directly at the window facing the cell tower.
  2. Tap '📐 Blueprint'. Select Room A, Room B, or Room C at the top.
  3. Touch and drag the Yellow Router Icon (📡) to test different spots in that room.
  4. Tap '🏆 Compare All Rooms' to view the leaderboard rankings and see which room provides peak throughput.
  5. Physical Motion Warning: If you switch room tabs without walking (> 3m moved), a warning alerts you to walk to that room for an accurate reading.

• Step 4 (📶 Cellular Signal Coverage & Extension Map)

  1. Tap '📶 Cellular Coverage'. Walk to the room or space furthest from your primary router.
  2. Tap '📶 Test Room Reception' to scan live signal quality:
    • Flashing Neon Green Diagram: '🔥 EXCELLENT RECEPTION (No extension nodes needed)'.
    • Amber Diagram: '⚡ GOOD RECEPTION (Move router 30cm closer to window)'.
    • Red Diagram: '⚠️ WEAK SIGNAL DEAD-ZONE! FITMENT OF A CELLULAR EXTENDER / MESH NODE RECOMMENDED HERE.'

🛠 PART 3: EXTRA TOOLS (ADVANCED PRO DIAGNOSTICS)

Tap '🛠 Extra Tools' on the top menu bar to access advanced diagnostic tools:

1. 📡 Nearby Alternative Cell Towers Map

Displays an OpenStreetMap showing all 4 nearby cell towers (Primary + 3 Alternatives). Tap any cell tower pin on the map to inspect its frequency band, distance, signal power (dBm), and alignment recommendations.

2. 📱 How to Select Bands & Change Towers on Mobile Networks Screen

Tapping '⚙️ Switch Band / Network' opens your phone's Mobile Networks settings screen. Follow these steps to force your phone onto an alternative cell tower:

• Method 1: Change 'Network Mode' (Switches Between 5G, 4G LTE, and 3G Towers)

  1. Tap 'Network Mode' on the Mobile Networks screen.
  2. You will see options like:
    • 5G / LTE / 3G / 2G (Auto connect) [Default]
    • LTE / 3G / 2G (Auto connect)
    • LTE Only
    • 3G Only
  3. How this switches towers: Primary 5G cell towers broadcast on high-frequency 5G C-Band (3,500 MHz). Alternative backup towers broadcast on long-range 4G LTE (800 MHz) or 3G.
    • Selecting 'LTE Only' forces your modem off the 5G tower and locks onto the Alternative 4G LTE Tower!
    • Selecting '3G Only' forces your modem onto the Alternative 3G Tower!

• Method 2: Use 'Network Operators' (Manual Cell Tower Search)

  1. Tap 'Network Operators' at the bottom of the Mobile Networks screen.
  2. Turn OFF the switch that says 'Select Automatically'.
  3. Your phone will perform a 30-second live search of every cell tower broadcasting in your area.
  4. A list of all nearby cell towers and carriers will appear. Tap any alternative tower/network in the list to force your phone to register onto it!

3. 🎯 5G/LTE Router Antenna Alignment Guide

Tapping '🎯 Router Antenna Guide' displays the exact azimuth bearing angle (e.g., 318° NW) to rotate outdoor Yagi/MIMO antennas to lock 5G home gateways onto alternative towers.

4. 🗺 3D Room Heatmap Matrix (RF Interference Grid)

  • How to Use: Walk across your room while the 3D grid measures RF signal attenuation.
  • Grid Color Scale: Green = Clear path; Yellow = Moderate attenuation; Red = Severe barrier loss.
  • Material Loss Values: Concrete Wall (-18 dB), Metal Foil Insulation (-30 dB), Steel Rebar (-22 dB), Glass (-2 dB).

5. 🔊 32-Band FFT Microphone Spectrum Analyzer

  • How to Use: Samples real-time audio room acoustic resonance across 32 frequency bands (20 Hz - 20 kHz).
  • How to Read: Identifies peak resonance (Hz), sound level (dB), and standing wave echo chambers caused by tile/glass surfaces.
