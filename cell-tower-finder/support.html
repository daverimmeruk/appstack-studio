🗑️ Account & Data Deletion Policy

Because Cell Tower Finder does not require user accounts, passwords, or cloud registrations, no user data is stored on remote servers.

What the app saves (tower positions, your readings with the positions where they were taken, Blueprint rooms, your Pro status and settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. To completely erase all app data from your device:

  1. Open your device Settings app.
  2. Go to Apps > Cell Tower Finder.
  3. Tap Storage > Clear storage (some phones say Clear data).
  4. Or uninstall the application.

📊 Google Play Data Safety Disclosures

  • Data Collected: None. Your readings, positions and settings stay on your phone and are not sent to us.
  • Data Shared: None. The app only downloads map pictures from OpenStreetMap and, when you run it, speed-test data from Cloudflare; like any website, those servers see your IP address. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Data Security: Data is kept in the app's private storage inside Android's sandbox, and the map and speed test use encrypted HTTPS connections.
