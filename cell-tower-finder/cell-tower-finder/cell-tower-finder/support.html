🗑️ Account & Data Deletion Policy

Because Cell Tower Finder v4.0 & WaveScan Pro does not require user accounts, passwords, or cloud registrations, no user data is stored on remote servers.

All local settings (such as subscription state or preferences) are stored strictly inside your phone's standard local application storage. To completely erase all app data from your device:

  1. Open your device Settings app.
  2. Navigate to Apps & Notifications > Cell Tower Finder v4.0.
  3. Tap Storage & Cache > Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  4. Uninstall the application.

📊 Google Play Data Safety Disclosures

  • Data Collected: None. No personal information is gathered or transmitted.
  • Data Shared: None. No data is shared with third parties.
  • Data Security: All processing occurs entirely in local memory (RAM) and securely within Android's sandboxed environment.
