1. Overview & Data Philosophy

AppStack Studio built Cell Tower Finder as a privacy-first utility application. All signal, location, camera and sound measurements are worked out on your phone, and what the app saves is kept on your phone. We have no servers, the app needs no account, and it has no ads or analytics. We do not sell or share your data. The only times the app uses the internet are listed in section 4.

2. Permissions Requested & On-Device Purpose

• Location Access (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)

Used on your phone to show your position on the map, to read the details of the cell your phone is using (Android requires location for this), and to work out the distance and direction to a tower. Tower positions you enter, and towers located from your own readings (each reading with the GPS position where it was taken), are saved on your phone so the map and AR view can show them. They are never uploaded.

• Wi-Fi and Network State (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Used to read the signal strength and link speed of the Wi-Fi network you're connected to (for Coverage and Blueprint), and to check whether you're online. Wi-Fi details are not uploaded.

• Camera Access (CAMERA)

Used strictly on-device to render the CameraX live Augmented Reality (AR) overlay, displaying 3D vector arrows pointing to cell towers and positioning 3D indoor router anchors. Video feeds are processed frame-by-frame in device memory and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

• Microphone / Audio Access (RECORD_AUDIO)

Used strictly on-device to calculate real-time 32-band FFT acoustic frequency resonance (20 Hz - 20 kHz) to identify standing wave echo chambers. Audio signals are converted immediately into frequency band numerical data in local RAM and discarded instantly. No voice recordings or raw audio files are ever saved or transmitted.

3. Local Storage & File Exports

Tower positions, your readings, Blueprint rooms and settings are kept in the app's private storage on your phone. Android's own backup (if you have it switched on) may include this app data in your Google account backup, like other apps' data.

When you export a report (PDF or JSON), the file is made in the app's private storage and only shared with the app or person you choose on Android's share screen, using Android's secure FileProvider API.

Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Cell Tower Finder › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored on your phone.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

  • Internet (INTERNET): used only for the three services below.
  • Osmdroid (OpenStreetMap): map pictures (tiles) are downloaded from OpenStreetMap's servers over encrypted HTTPS. Like any website, those servers receive your IP address and which map area is loaded.
  • Speed test (Cloudflare): only when you run the speed test, test data (up to about 30 MB) is downloaded from and uploaded to Cloudflare's public speed-test service, speed.cloudflare.com. Like any website, Cloudflare receives your IP address. No other data is sent.
  • Google Play Billing API: Handles the secure one-time purchase that unlocks Cell Tower Finder Pro. All payment processing is strictly managed by Google Play. We do not collect or store financial or credit card information. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used (for example whether a request succeeded or failed) and about connection problems, which Google uses to improve the library and help with errors. See Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Children’s Privacy

Our application is intended for general technical audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
