Doc Scan Buddy — Questions & Answers

1. Why doesn't the app ask for the camera?

The scanning screen is Google's document scanner, part of Google Play services. It takes the pictures and hands the app only the pages you save, so the app itself doesn't need camera permission.

2. The scanner doesn't open, or says it isn't ready.

The first time, Android may need a moment and an internet connection to get the scanner ready. Make sure Google Play services is up to date, connect once, then try again.

3. How do I scan a photo I already took?

Tap 📷 Scan a document, then the scanner's gallery button, and pick the photo. The scanner finds the edges and straightens it like a live scan.

4. The text has mistakes.

Reading the text (Pro) works best on clear printed text in good light. Handwriting, faded print and creased paper can come out with mistakes, so check anything important, like amounts and reference numbers.

5. Where are my documents kept?

Only in the app's own storage on your phone. They are not in Android's backups, so share or save anything you want to keep before uninstalling or changing phones.

6. I bought Pro on another phone. How do I get it back?

Use the same Google account, tap GO PRO at the top, then Restore purchase.
