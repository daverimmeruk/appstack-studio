Doc Scan Buddy — Privacy Policy

Package Name: com.appstackstudio.docscanbuddy | Effective Date: 26 November 2026 | Developer: AppStack Studio

1. Overview

Doc Scan Buddy turns letters, receipts, forms and notes into PDFs on your phone. Your documents stay on your phone. We have no servers, and we do not collect, sell or share your data. The app needs no account and has no ads and no analytics. (Google Play services and Google Play's billing library send Google their own diagnostic information, see section 5.)

2. Information Stored on Your Phone Only

  • Documents: the PDF of each scan, a picture of each page, the name you give it, the date it was made and the number of pages.
  • Text (Pro): the words read from a document's pages, once you use 🔤 Read the text.
  • Settings: whether the welcome guide was shown, whether Pro is unlocked, and how many useful results you have had (used only to decide when to ask for a Google Play rating).

3. Scanning and Reading the Text

The scanning screen is Google's document scanner (Google ML Kit), part of Google Play services on your phone. It runs on the phone: the app only receives the pages you save, never other photos, and has no camera or storage permission. When you import a page from your gallery, Android's picker gives the scanner only the pictures you choose.

Reading the text (Pro) also uses Google ML Kit on the phone. The pages and the text are not sent to Google or to us. The first time, Google Play services may download the scanner or the text reader to your phone.

4. Permissions Used

  • Google Play Billing: to handle the Pro purchase. Android also lists INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE because Google Play's billing library needs them; the app itself sends nothing over the internet.
  • No camera, storage or notification permission, no location, contacts, microphone, SMS or call log, no overlay, no accessibility service and no VPN.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google ML Kit (in Google Play services): the document scanner and the text reader. Google states that ML Kit may send Google information about how well these features work (for example which device and how long a task took), but not the pages or the text. See Google's ML Kit terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Billing: if you buy Pro (a one-time lifetime purchase), the payment is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment details. Google Play's billing library also sends Google its own diagnostic logs about how the purchase connection is used and about connection problems.
  • Google Play rating request: after a few useful results (for example a document saved or shared), the app may ask Google Play to show its review box. Google decides whether it appears; the app never sees your review.

6. Sharing

Your documents only leave your phone when you share them yourself: a document's PDF, its pages as pictures (Pro), its text (Pro), or the manual. They are then sent only to the app or person you choose on Android's share screen.

7. Keeping and Deleting Your Data

  • Delete a document with its pages and text by tapping ⋯ More, then 🗑 Delete.
  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings › Apps › Doc Scan Buddy › Storage › Clear storage, deletes everything the app has stored. The data is not included in Android's backups.
  • We never receive your data, so we hold nothing about you to delete.

8. Children

Doc Scan Buddy collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
